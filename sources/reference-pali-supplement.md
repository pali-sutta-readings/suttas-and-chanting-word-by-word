## 03_reflections/20_mettanisamsa_sutta.md

*AN 11.15* — Mettāsutta

[an11.15:1.1] “Mettāya, bhikkhave, cetovimuttiyā āsevitāya bhāvitāya bahulīkatāya yānīkatāya vatthukatāya anuṭṭhitāya paricitāya susamāraddhāya ekādasānisaṁsā pāṭikaṅkhā.  
[an11.15:2.1] Katame ekādasa?  
[an11.15:2.2] Sukhaṁ supati, sukhaṁ paṭibujjhati, na pāpakaṁ supinaṁ passati, manussānaṁ piyo hoti, amanussānaṁ piyo hoti, devatā rakkhanti, nāssa aggi vā visaṁ vā satthaṁ vā kamati, tuvaṭaṁ cittaṁ samādhiyati, mukhavaṇṇo vippasīdati, asammūḷho kālaṁ karoti, uttari appaṭivijjhanto brahmalokūpago hoti.  
[an11.15:2.3] Mettāya, bhikkhave, cetovimuttiyā āsevitāya bhāvitāya bahulīkatāya yānīkatāya vatthukatāya anuṭṭhitāya paricitāya susamāraddhāya ime ekādasānisaṁsā pāṭikaṅkhā”ti.  
[an11.15:2.4] Pañcamaṁ.

*AN 10.58* — Mūlakasutta

[an10.58:1.1] “Sace, bhikkhave, aññatitthiyā paribbājakā evaṁ puccheyyuṁ:  
[an10.58:1.2] ‘kiṁmūlakā, āvuso, sabbe dhammā, kiṁsambhavā sabbe dhammā, kiṁsamudayā sabbe dhammā, kiṁsamosaraṇā sabbe dhammā, kiṁpamukhā sabbe dhammā, kiṁadhipateyyā sabbe dhammā, kiṁuttarā sabbe dhammā, kiṁsārā sabbe dhammā, kiṁogadhā sabbe dhammā, kiṁpariyosānā sabbe dhammā’ti, evaṁ puṭṭhā tumhe, bhikkhave, tesaṁ aññatitthiyānaṁ paribbājakānaṁ kinti byākareyyāthā”ti?  
[an10.58:1.3] “Bhagavaṁmūlakā no, bhante, dhammā bhagavaṁnettikā bhagavaṁpaṭisaraṇā. Sādhu vata, bhante, bhagavantaṁyeva paṭibhātu etassa bhāsitassa attho. Bhagavato sutvā bhikkhū dhāressantī”ti.  
[an10.58:2.1] “Tena hi, bhikkhave, suṇātha, sādhukaṁ manasi karotha, bhāsissāmī”ti.  
[an10.58:2.2] “Evaṁ, bhante”ti kho te bhikkhū bhagavato paccassosuṁ.  
[an10.58:2.3] Bhagavā etadavoca:  
[an10.58:3.1] “Sace, bhikkhave, aññatitthiyā paribbājakā evaṁ puccheyyuṁ:  
[an10.58:3.2] ‘kiṁmūlakā, āvuso, sabbe dhammā, kiṁsambhavā sabbe dhammā, kiṁsamudayā sabbe dhammā, kiṁsamosaraṇā sabbe dhammā, kiṁpamukhā sabbe dhammā, kiṁ adhipateyyā sabbe dhammā, kiṁuttarā sabbe dhammā, kiṁsārā sabbe dhammā, kiṁogadhā sabbe dhammā, kiṁpariyosānā sabbe dhammā’ti,  
[an10.58:3.3] evaṁ puṭṭhā tumhe, bhikkhave, tesaṁ aññatitthiyānaṁ paribbājakānaṁ evaṁ byākareyyātha:  
[an10.58:3.4] ‘chandamūlakā, āvuso, sabbe dhammā, manasikārasambhavā sabbe dhammā, phassasamudayā sabbe dhammā, vedanāsamosaraṇā sabbe dhammā, samādhippamukhā sabbe dhammā, satādhipateyyā sabbe dhammā, paññuttarā sabbe dhammā, vimuttisārā sabbe dhammā, amatogadhā sabbe dhammā, nibbānapariyosānā sabbe dhammā’ti.  
[an10.58:3.5] Evaṁ puṭṭhā tumhe, bhikkhave, tesaṁ aññatitthiyānaṁ paribbājakānaṁ evaṁ byākareyyāthā”ti.  
[an10.58:3.6] Aṭṭhamaṁ.

## 07a_suttas/12_itivuttaka_49_ditthigatasutta.md

*Iti 49* — Dutiyavagga

[iti49:1.1] Vuttañhetaṁ bhagavatā vuttamarahatāti me sutaṁ:  
[iti49:2.1] “Dvīhi, bhikkhave, diṭṭhigatehi pariyuṭṭhitā devamanussā olīyanti eke, atidhāvanti eke; cakkhumanto ca passanti.  
[iti49:3.1] Kathañca, bhikkhave, olīyanti eke?  
[iti49:3.2] Bhavārāmā, bhikkhave, devamanussā bhavaratā bhavasammuditā tesaṁ bhavanirodhāya dhamme desiyamāne cittaṁ na pakkhandati na pasīdati na santiṭṭhati nādhimuccati.  
[iti49:3.3] Evaṁ kho, bhikkhave, olīyanti eke.  
[iti49:4.1] Kathañca, bhikkhave, atidhāvanti eke?  
[iti49:4.2] Bhaveneva kho paneke aṭṭīyamānā harāyamānā jigucchamānā vibhavaṁ abhinandanti—  
[iti49:4.3] yato kira, bho, ayaṁ attā kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā ucchijjati vinassati na hoti paraṁ maraṇā;  
[iti49:4.4] etaṁ santaṁ etaṁ paṇītaṁ etaṁ yāthāvanti.  
[iti49:4.5] Evaṁ kho, bhikkhave, atidhāvanti eke.  
[iti49:5.1] Kathañca, bhikkhave, cakkhumanto passanti?  
[iti49:5.2] Idha bhikkhu bhūtaṁ bhūtato passati;  
[iti49:5.3] bhūtaṁ bhūtato disvā bhūtassa nibbidāya virāgāya nirodhāya paṭipanno hoti.  
[iti49:5.4] Evaṁ kho, bhikkhave, cakkhumanto passantī”ti.  
[iti49:5.5] Etamatthaṁ bhagavā avoca.  
[iti49:5.6] Tatthetaṁ iti vuccati:  
[iti49:6.1] “Ye bhūtaṁ bhūtato disvā,  
[iti49:6.2] bhūtassa ca atikkamaṁ;  
[iti49:6.3] Yathābhūte vimuccanti,  
[iti49:6.4] bhavataṇhā parikkhayā.  
[iti49:7.1] Sa ve bhūtapariñño so,  
[iti49:7.2] vītataṇho bhavābhave;  
[iti49:7.3] Bhūtassa vibhavā bhikkhu,  
[iti49:7.4] nāgacchati punabbhavan”ti.  
[iti49:8.1] Ayampi attho vutto bhagavatā, iti me sutanti.  
[iti49:8.2] Dvādasamaṁ.  
[iti49:8.3] Dutiyo vaggo.  
[iti49:9.0] Tassuddānaṁ  
[iti49:9.1] Dve indriyā dve tapanīyā,  
[iti49:9.2] sīlena apare duve;  
[iti49:9.3] Anottāpī kuhanā dve ca,  
[iti49:9.4] saṁvejanīyena te dasa.  
[iti49:10.1] Vitakkā desanā vijjā,  
[iti49:10.2] paññā dhammena pañcamaṁ;  
[iti49:10.3] Ajātaṁ dhātusallānaṁ,  
[iti49:10.4] sikkhā jāgariyena ca;  
[iti49:10.5] Apāyadiṭṭhiyā ceva,  
[iti49:10.6] bāvīsati pakāsitāti.  
[iti49:10.7] Dukanipāto niṭṭhito.  

## 07b_anagatabhaya_suttas/01_pathamaanagatabhayasutta.md

*AN 5.77* — Paṭhamaanāgatabhayasutta

[an5.77:1.1] “Pañcimāni, bhikkhave, anāgatabhayāni sampassamānena alameva āraññikena bhikkhunā appamattena ātāpinā pahitattena viharituṁ appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya.  
[an5.77:2.1] Katamāni pañca?  
[an5.77:2.2] Idha, bhikkhave, āraññiko bhikkhu iti paṭisañcikkhati:  
[an5.77:2.3] ‘ahaṁ kho etarahi ekako araññe viharāmi.  
[an5.77:2.4] Ekakaṁ kho pana maṁ araññe viharantaṁ  
[an5.77:2.5] ahi vā maṁ ḍaṁseyya, vicchiko vā maṁ ḍaṁseyya, satapadī vā maṁ ḍaṁseyya,  
[an5.77:2.6] tena me assa kālaṅkiriyā, so mamassa antarāyo;  
[an5.77:2.7] handāhaṁ vīriyaṁ ārabhāmi appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāyā’ti.  
[an5.77:2.8] Idaṁ, bhikkhave, paṭhamaṁ anāgatabhayaṁ sampassamānena alameva āraññikena bhikkhunā appamattena ātāpinā pahitattena viharituṁ appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya.  
[an5.77:3.1] Puna caparaṁ, bhikkhave, āraññiko bhikkhu iti paṭisañcikkhati:  
[an5.77:3.2] ‘ahaṁ kho etarahi ekako araññe viharāmi.  
[an5.77:3.3] Ekako kho panāhaṁ araññe viharanto  
[an5.77:3.4] upakkhalitvā vā papateyyaṁ, bhattaṁ vā bhuttaṁ me byāpajjeyya, pittaṁ vā me kuppeyya, semhaṁ vā me kuppeyya, satthakā vā me vātā kuppeyyuṁ,  
[an5.77:3.5] tena me assa kālaṅkiriyā, so mamassa antarāyo;  
[an5.77:3.6] handāhaṁ vīriyaṁ ārabhāmi appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāyā’ti.  
[an5.77:3.7] Idaṁ, bhikkhave, dutiyaṁ anāgatabhayaṁ sampassamānena alameva āraññikena bhikkhunā appamattena ātāpinā pahitattena viharituṁ appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya.  
[an5.77:4.1] Puna caparaṁ, bhikkhave, āraññiko bhikkhu iti paṭisañcikkhati:  
[an5.77:4.2] ‘ahaṁ kho etarahi ekako araññe viharāmi.  
[an5.77:4.3] Ekako kho panāhaṁ araññe viharanto  
[an5.77:4.4] vāḷehi samāgaccheyyaṁ, sīhena vā byagghena vā dīpinā vā acchena vā taracchena vā, te maṁ jīvitā voropeyyuṁ,  
[an5.77:4.5] tena me assa kālaṅkiriyā, so mamassa antarāyo;  
[an5.77:4.6] handāhaṁ vīriyaṁ ārabhāmi appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāyā’ti.  
[an5.77:4.7] Idaṁ, bhikkhave, tatiyaṁ anāgatabhayaṁ sampassamānena alameva āraññikena bhikkhunā appamattena ātāpinā pahitattena viharituṁ appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya.  
[an5.77:5.1] Puna caparaṁ, bhikkhave, āraññiko bhikkhu iti paṭisañcikkhati:  
[an5.77:5.2] ‘ahaṁ kho etarahi ekako araññe viharāmi.  
[an5.77:5.3] Ekako kho panāhaṁ araññe viharanto  
[an5.77:5.4] māṇavehi samāgaccheyyaṁ katakammehi vā akatakammehi vā, te maṁ jīvitā voropeyyuṁ,  
[an5.77:5.5] tena me assa kālaṅkiriyā, so mamassa antarāyo;  
[an5.77:5.6] handāhaṁ vīriyaṁ ārabhāmi appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāyā’ti.  
[an5.77:5.7] Idaṁ, bhikkhave, catutthaṁ anāgatabhayaṁ sampassamānena alameva āraññikena bhikkhunā appamattena ātāpinā pahitattena viharituṁ appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya.  
[an5.77:6.1] Puna caparaṁ, bhikkhave, āraññiko bhikkhu iti paṭisañcikkhati:  
[an5.77:6.2] ‘ahaṁ kho etarahi ekako araññe viharāmi.  
[an5.77:6.3] Santi kho panāraññe vāḷā amanussā, te maṁ jīvitā voropeyyuṁ,  
[an5.77:6.4] tena me assa kālaṅkiriyā, so mamassa antarāyo;  
[an5.77:6.5] handāhaṁ vīriyaṁ ārabhāmi appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāyā’ti.  
[an5.77:6.6] Idaṁ, bhikkhave, pañcamaṁ anāgatabhayaṁ sampassamānena alameva āraññikena bhikkhunā appamattena ātāpinā pahitattena viharituṁ appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya.  
[an5.77:7.1] Imāni kho, bhikkhave, pañca anāgatabhayāni sampassamānena alameva āraññikena bhikkhunā appamattena ātāpinā pahitattena viharituṁ appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāyā”ti.  
[an5.77:7.2] Sattamaṁ.  

## 07b_anagatabhaya_suttas/02_dutiyaanagatabhayasutta.md

*AN 5.78* — Dutiyaanāgatabhayasutta

[an5.78:1.1] “Pañcimāni, bhikkhave, anāgatabhayāni sampassamānena alameva bhikkhunā appamattena ātāpinā pahitattena viharituṁ appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya.  
[an5.78:1.2] Katamāni pañca?  
[an5.78:1.3] Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu iti paṭisañcikkhati:  
[an5.78:1.4] ‘ahaṁ kho etarahi daharo yuvā susukāḷakeso bhadrena yobbanena samannāgato paṭhamena vayasā.  
[an5.78:1.5] Hoti kho pana so samayo yaṁ imaṁ kāyaṁ jarā phusati.  
[an5.78:1.6] Jiṇṇena kho pana jarāya abhibhūtena na sukaraṁ buddhānaṁ sāsanaṁ manasi kātuṁ, na sukarāni araññavanapatthāni pantāni senāsanāni paṭisevituṁ.  
[an5.78:1.7] Purā maṁ so dhammo āgacchati aniṭṭho akanto amanāpo;  
[an5.78:1.8] handāhaṁ paṭikacceva vīriyaṁ ārabhāmi appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya, yenāhaṁ dhammena samannāgato jiṇṇakopi phāsuṁ viharissāmī’ti.  
[an5.78:1.9] Idaṁ, bhikkhave, paṭhamaṁ anāgatabhayaṁ sampassamānena alameva bhikkhunā appamattena ātāpinā pahitattena viharituṁ appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya.  
[an5.78:2.1] Puna caparaṁ, bhikkhave, bhikkhu iti paṭisañcikkhati:  
[an5.78:2.2] ‘ahaṁ kho etarahi appābādho appātaṅko samavepākiniyā gahaṇiyā samannāgato nātisītāya nāccuṇhāya majjhimāya padhānakkhamāya.  
[an5.78:2.3] Hoti kho pana so samayo yaṁ imaṁ kāyaṁ byādhi phusati.  
[an5.78:2.4] Byādhitena kho pana byādhinā abhibhūtena na sukaraṁ buddhānaṁ sāsanaṁ manasi kātuṁ, na sukarāni araññavanapatthāni pantāni senāsanāni paṭisevituṁ.  
[an5.78:2.5] Purā maṁ so dhammo āgacchati aniṭṭho akanto amanāpo;  
[an5.78:2.6] handāhaṁ paṭikacceva vīriyaṁ ārabhāmi appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya, yenāhaṁ dhammena samannāgato byādhitopi phāsuṁ viharissāmī’ti.  
[an5.78:2.7] Idaṁ, bhikkhave, dutiyaṁ anāgatabhayaṁ sampassamānena alameva bhikkhunā appamattena ātāpinā pahitattena viharituṁ appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya.  
[an5.78:3.1] Puna caparaṁ, bhikkhave, bhikkhu iti paṭisañcikkhati:  
[an5.78:3.2] ‘etarahi kho subhikkhaṁ susassaṁ sulabhapiṇḍaṁ, sukaraṁ uñchena paggahena yāpetuṁ.  
[an5.78:3.3] Hoti kho pana so samayo yaṁ dubbhikkhaṁ hoti dussassaṁ dullabhapiṇḍaṁ, na sukaraṁ uñchena paggahena yāpetuṁ.  
[an5.78:3.4] Dubbhikkhe kho pana manussā yena subhikkhaṁ tena saṅkamanti.  
[an5.78:3.5] Tattha saṅgaṇikavihāro hoti ākiṇṇavihāro.  
[an5.78:3.6] Saṅgaṇikavihāre kho pana sati ākiṇṇavihāre na sukaraṁ buddhānaṁ sāsanaṁ manasi kātuṁ, na sukarāni araññavanapatthāni pantāni senāsanāni paṭisevituṁ.  
[an5.78:3.7] Purā maṁ so dhammo āgacchati aniṭṭho akanto amanāpo;  
[an5.78:3.8] handāhaṁ paṭikacceva vīriyaṁ ārabhāmi appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya, yenāhaṁ dhammena samannāgato dubbhikkhepi phāsu viharissāmī’ti.  
[an5.78:3.9] Idaṁ, bhikkhave, tatiyaṁ anāgatabhayaṁ sampassamānena alameva bhikkhunā appamattena ātāpinā pahitattena viharituṁ appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya.  
[an5.78:4.1] Puna caparaṁ, bhikkhave, bhikkhu iti paṭisañcikkhati:  
[an5.78:4.2] ‘etarahi kho manussā samaggā sammodamānā avivadamānā khīrodakībhūtā aññamaññaṁ piyacakkhūhi sampassantā viharanti.  
[an5.78:4.3] Hoti kho pana so samayo yaṁ bhayaṁ hoti aṭavisaṅkopo, cakkasamārūḷhā jānapadā pariyāyanti.  
[an5.78:4.4] Bhaye kho pana sati manussā yena khemaṁ tena saṅkamanti.  
[an5.78:4.5] Tattha saṅgaṇikavihāro hoti ākiṇṇavihāro.  
[an5.78:4.6] Saṅgaṇikavihāre kho pana sati ākiṇṇavihāre na sukaraṁ buddhānaṁ sāsanaṁ manasi kātuṁ, na sukarāni araññavanapatthāni pantāni senāsanāni paṭisevituṁ.  
[an5.78:4.7] Purā maṁ so dhammo āgacchati aniṭṭho akanto amanāpo;  
[an5.78:4.8] handāhaṁ paṭikacceva vīriyaṁ ārabhāmi appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya, yenāhaṁ dhammena samannāgato bhayepi phāsuṁ viharissāmī’ti.  
[an5.78:4.9] Idaṁ, bhikkhave, catutthaṁ anāgatabhayaṁ sampassamānena alameva bhikkhunā appamattena ātāpinā pahitattena viharituṁ appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya.  
[an5.78:5.1] Puna caparaṁ, bhikkhave, bhikkhu iti paṭisañcikkhati:  
[an5.78:5.2] ‘etarahi kho saṅgho samaggo sammodamāno avivadamāno ekuddeso phāsu viharati.  
[an5.78:5.3] Hoti kho pana so samayo yaṁ saṅgho bhijjati.  
[an5.78:5.4] Saṅghe kho pana bhinne na sukaraṁ buddhānaṁ sāsanaṁ manasi kātuṁ, na sukarāni araññavanapatthāni pantāni senāsanāni paṭisevituṁ.  
[an5.78:5.5] Purā maṁ so dhammo āgacchati aniṭṭho akanto amanāpo;  
[an5.78:5.6] handāhaṁ paṭikacceva vīriyaṁ ārabhāmi appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya, yenāhaṁ dhammena samannāgato bhinnepi saṅghe phāsuṁ viharissāmī’ti.  
[an5.78:5.7] Idaṁ, bhikkhave, pañcamaṁ anāgatabhayaṁ sampassamānena alameva bhikkhunā appamattena ātāpinā pahitattena viharituṁ appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya.  
[an5.78:6.1] Imāni kho, bhikkhave, pañca anāgatabhayāni sampassamānena alameva bhikkhunā appamattena ātāpinā pahitattena viharituṁ appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāyā”ti.  
[an5.78:6.2] Aṭṭhamaṁ.  

## 07b_anagatabhaya_suttas/03_tatiyaanagatabhayasutta.md

*AN 5.79* — Tatiyaanāgatabhayasutta

[an5.79:1.1] “Pañcimāni, bhikkhave, anāgatabhayāni etarahi asamuppannāni āyatiṁ samuppajjissanti.  
[an5.79:1.2] Tāni vo paṭibujjhitabbāni;  
[an5.79:1.3] paṭibujjhitvā ca tesaṁ pahānāya vāyamitabbaṁ.  
[an5.79:2.1] Katamāni pañca?  
[an5.79:2.2] Bhavissanti, bhikkhave, bhikkhū anāgatamaddhānaṁ abhāvitakāyā abhāvitasīlā abhāvitacittā abhāvitapaññā.  
[an5.79:2.3] Te abhāvitakāyā samānā abhāvitasīlā abhāvitacittā abhāvitapaññā aññe upasampādessanti.  
[an5.79:2.4] Tepi na sakkhissanti vinetuṁ adhisīle adhicitte adhipaññāya.  
[an5.79:2.5] Tepi bhavissanti abhāvitakāyā abhāvitasīlā abhāvitacittā abhāvitapaññā.  
[an5.79:2.6] Te abhāvitakāyā samānā abhāvitasīlā abhāvitacittā abhāvitapaññā aññe upasampādessanti.  
[an5.79:2.7] Tepi na sakkhissanti vinetuṁ adhisīle adhicitte adhipaññāya.  
[an5.79:2.8] Tepi bhavissanti abhāvitakāyā abhāvitasīlā abhāvitacittā abhāvitapaññā.  
[an5.79:2.9] Iti kho, bhikkhave, dhammasandosā vinayasandoso;  
[an5.79:2.10] vinayasandosā dhammasandoso.  
[an5.79:2.11] Idaṁ, bhikkhave, paṭhamaṁ anāgatabhayaṁ etarahi asamuppannaṁ āyatiṁ samuppajjissati.  
[an5.79:2.12] Taṁ vo paṭibujjhitabbaṁ;  
[an5.79:2.13] paṭibujjhitvā ca tassa pahānāya vāyamitabbaṁ.  
[an5.79:3.1] Puna caparaṁ, bhikkhave, bhavissanti bhikkhū anāgatamaddhānaṁ abhāvitakāyā abhāvitasīlā abhāvitacittā abhāvitapaññā.  
[an5.79:3.2] Te abhāvitakāyā samānā abhāvitasīlā abhāvitacittā abhāvitapaññā aññesaṁ nissayaṁ dassanti.  
[an5.79:3.3] Tepi na sakkhissanti vinetuṁ adhisīle adhicitte adhipaññāya.  
[an5.79:3.4] Tepi bhavissanti abhāvitakāyā abhāvitasīlā abhāvitacittā abhāvitapaññā.  
[an5.79:3.5] Te abhāvitakāyā samānā abhāvitasīlā abhāvitacittā abhāvitapaññā aññesaṁ nissayaṁ dassanti.  
[an5.79:3.6] Tepi na sakkhissanti vinetuṁ adhisīle adhicitte adhipaññāya.  
[an5.79:3.7] Tepi bhavissanti abhāvitakāyā abhāvitasīlā abhāvitacittā abhāvitapaññā.  
[an5.79:3.8] Iti kho, bhikkhave, dhammasandosā vinayasandoso;  
[an5.79:3.9] vinayasandosā dhammasandoso.  
[an5.79:3.10] Idaṁ, bhikkhave, dutiyaṁ anāgatabhayaṁ etarahi asamuppannaṁ āyatiṁ samuppajjissati.  
[an5.79:3.11] Taṁ vo paṭibujjhitabbaṁ;  
[an5.79:3.12] paṭibujjhitvā ca tassa pahānāya vāyamitabbaṁ.  
[an5.79:4.1] Puna caparaṁ, bhikkhave, bhavissanti bhikkhū anāgatamaddhānaṁ abhāvitakāyā abhāvitasīlā abhāvitacittā abhāvitapaññā.  
[an5.79:4.2] Te abhāvitakāyā samānā abhāvitasīlā abhāvitacittā abhāvitapaññā abhidhammakathaṁ vedallakathaṁ kathentā kaṇhadhammaṁ okkamamānā na bujjhissanti.  
[an5.79:4.3] Iti kho, bhikkhave, dhammasandosā vinayasandoso;  
[an5.79:4.4] vinayasandosā dhammasandoso.  
[an5.79:4.5] Idaṁ, bhikkhave, tatiyaṁ anāgatabhayaṁ etarahi asamuppannaṁ āyatiṁ samuppajjissati.  
[an5.79:4.6] Taṁ vo paṭibujjhitabbaṁ;  
[an5.79:4.7] paṭibujjhitvā ca tassa pahānāya vāyamitabbaṁ.  
[an5.79:5.1] Puna caparaṁ, bhikkhave, bhavissanti bhikkhū anāgatamaddhānaṁ abhāvitakāyā abhāvitasīlā abhāvitacittā abhāvitapaññā.  
[an5.79:5.2] Te abhāvitakāyā samānā abhāvitasīlā abhāvitacittā abhāvitapaññā ye te suttantā tathāgatabhāsitā gambhīrā gambhīratthā lokuttarā suññatāppaṭisaṁyuttā, tesu bhaññamānesu na sussūsissanti, na sotaṁ odahissanti, na aññā cittaṁ upaṭṭhapessanti, na ca te dhamme uggahetabbaṁ pariyāpuṇitabbaṁ maññissanti.  
[an5.79:5.3] Ye pana te suttantā kavitā kāveyyā cittakkharā cittabyañjanā bāhirakā sāvakabhāsitā, tesu bhaññamānesu sussūsissanti, sotaṁ odahissanti, aññā cittaṁ upaṭṭhapessanti, te ca dhamme uggahetabbaṁ pariyāpuṇitabbaṁ maññissanti.  
[an5.79:5.4] Iti kho, bhikkhave, dhammasandosā vinayasandoso;  
[an5.79:5.5] vinayasandosā dhammasandoso.  
[an5.79:5.6] Idaṁ, bhikkhave, catutthaṁ anāgatabhayaṁ etarahi asamuppannaṁ āyatiṁ samuppajjissati.  
[an5.79:5.7] Taṁ vo paṭibujjhitabbaṁ;  
[an5.79:5.8] paṭibujjhitvā ca tassa pahānāya vāyamitabbaṁ.  
[an5.79:6.1] Puna caparaṁ, bhikkhave, bhavissanti bhikkhū anāgatamaddhānaṁ abhāvitakāyā abhāvitasīlā abhāvitacittā abhāvitapaññā.  
[an5.79:6.2] Te abhāvitakāyā samānā abhāvitasīlā abhāvitacittā abhāvitapaññā therā bhikkhū bāhulikā bhavissanti sāthalikā okkamane pubbaṅgamā paviveke nikkhittadhurā, na vīriyaṁ ārabhissanti appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya.  
[an5.79:6.3] Tesaṁ pacchimā janatā diṭṭhānugatiṁ āpajjissati.  
[an5.79:6.4] Sāpi bhavissati bāhulikā sāthalikā okkamane pubbaṅgamā paviveke nikkhittadhurā, na vīriyaṁ ārabhissati appattassa pattiyā anadhigatassa adhigamāya asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya.  
[an5.79:6.5] Iti kho, bhikkhave, dhammasandosā vinayasandoso;  
[an5.79:6.6] vinayasandosā dhammasandoso.  
[an5.79:6.7] Idaṁ, bhikkhave, pañcamaṁ anāgatabhayaṁ etarahi asamuppannaṁ āyatiṁ samuppajjissati.  
[an5.79:6.8] Taṁ vo paṭibujjhitabbaṁ;  
[an5.79:6.9] paṭibujjhitvā ca tassa pahānāya vāyamitabbaṁ.  
[an5.79:7.1] Imāni kho, bhikkhave, pañca anāgatabhayāni etarahi asamuppannāni āyatiṁ samuppajjissanti.  
[an5.79:7.2] Tāni vo paṭibujjhitabbāni;  
[an5.79:7.3] paṭibujjhitvā ca tesaṁ pahānāya vāyamitabban”ti.  
[an5.79:7.4] Navamaṁ.

*AN 5.80* — Catutthaanāgatabhayasutta

[an5.80:1.1] “Pañcimāni, bhikkhave, anāgatabhayāni etarahi asamuppannāni āyatiṁ samuppajjissanti.  
[an5.80:1.2] Tāni vo paṭibujjhitabbāni;  
[an5.80:1.3] paṭibujjhitvā ca tesaṁ pahānāya vāyamitabbaṁ.  
[an5.80:2.1] Katamāni pañca?  
[an5.80:2.2] Bhavissanti, bhikkhave, bhikkhū anāgatamaddhānaṁ cīvare kalyāṇakāmā.  
[an5.80:2.3] Te cīvare kalyāṇakāmā samānā riñcissanti paṁsukūlikattaṁ, riñcissanti araññavanapatthāni pantāni senāsanāni;  
[an5.80:2.4] gāmanigamarājadhānīsu osaritvā vāsaṁ kappessanti, cīvarahetu ca anekavihitaṁ anesanaṁ appatirūpaṁ āpajjissanti.  
[an5.80:2.5] Idaṁ, bhikkhave, paṭhamaṁ anāgatabhayaṁ etarahi asamuppannaṁ āyatiṁ samuppajjissati.  
[an5.80:2.6] Taṁ vo paṭibujjhitabbaṁ;  
[an5.80:2.7] paṭibujjhitvā ca tassa pahānāya vāyamitabbaṁ.  
[an5.80:3.1] Puna caparaṁ, bhikkhave, bhavissanti bhikkhū anāgatamaddhānaṁ piṇḍapāte kalyāṇakāmā.  
[an5.80:3.2] Te piṇḍapāte kalyāṇakāmā samānā riñcissanti piṇḍapātikattaṁ, riñcissanti araññavanapatthāni pantāni senāsanāni;  
[an5.80:3.3] gāmanigamarājadhānīsu osaritvā vāsaṁ kappessanti jivhaggena rasaggāni pariyesamānā, piṇḍapātahetu ca anekavihitaṁ anesanaṁ appatirūpaṁ āpajjissanti.  
[an5.80:3.4] Idaṁ, bhikkhave, dutiyaṁ anāgatabhayaṁ etarahi asamuppannaṁ āyatiṁ samuppajjissati.  
[an5.80:3.5] Taṁ vo paṭibujjhitabbaṁ;  
[an5.80:3.6] paṭibujjhitvā ca tassa pahānāya vāyamitabbaṁ.  
[an5.80:4.1] Puna caparaṁ, bhikkhave, bhavissanti bhikkhū anāgatamaddhānaṁ senāsane kalyāṇakāmā.  
[an5.80:4.2] Te senāsane kalyāṇakāmā samānā riñcissanti rukkhamūlikattaṁ, riñcissanti araññavanapatthāni pantāni senāsanāni;  
[an5.80:4.3] gāmanigamarājadhānīsu osaritvā vāsaṁ kappessanti, senāsanahetu ca anekavihitaṁ anesanaṁ appatirūpaṁ āpajjissanti.  
[an5.80:4.4] Idaṁ, bhikkhave, tatiyaṁ anāgatabhayaṁ etarahi asamuppannaṁ āyatiṁ samuppajjissati.  
[an5.80:4.5] Taṁ vo paṭibujjhitabbaṁ;  
[an5.80:4.6] paṭibujjhitvā ca tassa pahānāya vāyamitabbaṁ.  
[an5.80:5.1] Puna caparaṁ, bhikkhave, bhavissanti bhikkhū anāgatamaddhānaṁ bhikkhunīsikkhamānāsamaṇuddesehi saṁsaṭṭhā viharissanti.  
[an5.80:5.2] Bhikkhunīsikkhamānāsamaṇuddesehi saṁsagge kho pana, bhikkhave, sati etaṁ pāṭikaṅkhaṁ:  
[an5.80:5.3] ‘anabhiratā vā brahmacariyaṁ carissanti, aññataraṁ vā saṅkiliṭṭhaṁ āpattiṁ āpajjissanti, sikkhaṁ vā paccakkhāya hīnāyāvattissanti’.  
[an5.80:5.4] Idaṁ, bhikkhave, catutthaṁ anāgatabhayaṁ etarahi asamuppannaṁ āyatiṁ samuppajjissati.  
[an5.80:5.5] Taṁ vo paṭibujjhitabbaṁ;  
[an5.80:5.6] paṭibujjhitvā ca tassa pahānāya vāyamitabbaṁ.  
[an5.80:6.1] Puna caparaṁ, bhikkhave, bhavissanti bhikkhū anāgatamaddhānaṁ ārāmikasamaṇuddesehi saṁsaṭṭhā viharissanti.  
[an5.80:6.2] Ārāmikasamaṇuddesehi saṁsagge kho pana, bhikkhave, sati etaṁ pāṭikaṅkhaṁ:  
[an5.80:6.3] ‘anekavihitaṁ sannidhikāraparibhogaṁ anuyuttā viharissanti, oḷārikampi nimittaṁ karissanti, pathaviyāpi haritaggepi’.  
[an5.80:6.4] Idaṁ, bhikkhave, pañcamaṁ anāgatabhayaṁ etarahi asamuppannaṁ āyatiṁ samuppajjissati.  
[an5.80:6.5] Taṁ vo paṭibujjhitabbaṁ;  
[an5.80:6.6] paṭibujjhitvā ca tassa pahānāya vāyamitabbaṁ.

[an5.80:7.2] Tāni vo paṭibujjhitabbāni;  
[an5.80:7.3] paṭibujjhitvā ca tesaṁ pahānāya vāyamitabban”ti.  
[an5.80:7.4] Dasamaṁ.

## 07b_anagatabhaya_suttas/05_saddhammappatirupakasutta.md

*SN 16.13* — Saddhammappatirūpakasutta

[sn16.13:1.1] Evaṁ me sutaṁ—  
[sn16.13:1.2] ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme.  
[sn16.13:1.3] Atha kho āyasmā mahākassapo yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṁ abhivādetvā ekamantaṁ nisīdi. Ekamantaṁ nisinno kho āyasmā mahākassapo bhagavantaṁ etadavoca:  
[sn16.13:1.4] “ko nu kho, bhante, hetu ko paccayo, yena pubbe appatarāni ceva sikkhāpadāni ahesuṁ bahutarā ca bhikkhū aññāya saṇṭhahiṁsu?  
[sn16.13:1.5] Ko pana, bhante, hetu ko paccayo, yenetarahi bahutarāni ceva sikkhāpadāni appatarā ca bhikkhū aññāya saṇṭhahantī”ti?  
[sn16.13:1.6] “Evañcetaṁ, kassapa, hoti sattesu hāyamānesu saddhamme antaradhāyamāne, bahutarāni ceva sikkhāpadāni honti appatarā ca bhikkhū aññāya saṇṭhahanti.  
[sn16.13:1.7] Na tāva, kassapa, saddhammassa antaradhānaṁ hoti yāva na saddhammappatirūpakaṁ loke uppajjati.  
[sn16.13:1.8] Yato ca kho, kassapa, saddhammappatirūpakaṁ loke uppajjati, atha saddhammassa antaradhānaṁ hoti.  
[sn16.13:2.1] Seyyathāpi, kassapa, na tāva jātarūpassa antaradhānaṁ hoti yāva na jātarūpappatirūpakaṁ loke uppajjati.  
[sn16.13:2.2] Yato ca kho, kassapa, jātarūpappatirūpakaṁ loke uppajjati, atha kho jātarūpassa antaradhānaṁ hoti.  
[sn16.13:2.3] Evameva kho, kassapa, na tāva saddhammassa antaradhānaṁ hoti yāva na saddhammappatirūpakaṁ loke uppajjati.  
[sn16.13:2.4] Yato ca kho, kassapa, saddhammappatirūpakaṁ loke uppajjati, atha saddhammassa antaradhānaṁ hoti.  
[sn16.13:3.1] Na kho, kassapa, pathavīdhātu saddhammaṁ antaradhāpeti, na āpodhātu saddhammaṁ antaradhāpeti, na tejodhātu saddhammaṁ antaradhāpeti, na vāyodhātu saddhammaṁ antaradhāpeti;  
[sn16.13:3.2] atha kho idheva te uppajjanti moghapurisā ye imaṁ saddhammaṁ antaradhāpenti.  
[sn16.13:3.3] Seyyathāpi, kassapa, nāvā ādikeneva opilavati;  
[sn16.13:3.4] na kho, kassapa, evaṁ saddhammassa antaradhānaṁ hoti.  
[sn16.13:4.1] Pañca khome, kassapa, okkamaniyā dhammā saddhammassa sammosāya antaradhānāya saṁvattanti.  
[sn16.13:4.2] Katame pañca?  
[sn16.13:4.3] Idha, kassapa, bhikkhū bhikkhuniyo upāsakā upāsikāyo satthari agāravā viharanti appatissā, dhamme agāravā viharanti appatissā, saṅghe agāravā viharanti appatissā, sikkhāya agāravā viharanti appatissā, samādhismiṁ agāravā viharanti appatissā—  
[sn16.13:4.4] ime kho, kassapa, pañca okkamaniyā dhammā saddhammassa sammosāya antaradhānāya saṁvattanti.  
[sn16.13:5.1] Pañca khome, kassapa, dhammā saddhammassa ṭhitiyā asammosāya anantaradhānāya saṁvattanti.  
[sn16.13:5.2] Katame pañca?  
[sn16.13:5.3] Idha, kassapa, bhikkhū bhikkhuniyo upāsakā upāsikāyo satthari sagāravā viharanti sappatissā, dhamme sagāravā viharanti sappatissā, saṅghe sagāravā viharanti sappatissā, sikkhāya sagāravā viharanti sappatissā, samādhismiṁ sagāravā viharanti sappatissā—  
[sn16.13:5.4] ime kho, kassapa, pañca dhammā saddhammassa ṭhitiyā asammosāya anantaradhānāya saṁvattantī”ti.  
[sn16.13:5.5] Terasamaṁ.  
[sn16.13:5.6] Kassapavaggo paṭhamo.  
[sn16.13:6.0] Tassuddānaṁ  
[sn16.13:6.1] Santuṭṭhañca anottappī,  
[sn16.13:6.2] candūpamaṁ kulūpakaṁ;  
[sn16.13:6.3] Jiṇṇaṁ tayo ca ovādā,  
[sn16.13:6.4] jhānābhiññā upassayaṁ;  
[sn16.13:6.5] Cīvaraṁ paraṁmaraṇaṁ,  
[sn16.13:6.6] saddhammappatirūpakanti.  
[sn16.13:6.7] Kassapasaṁyuttaṁ samattaṁ.  

## 07b_anagatabhaya_suttas/06_kimilasutta.md

*AN 7.59* — Kimilasutta

[an7.59:1.1] Evaṁ me sutaṁ—  
[an7.59:1.2] ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā kimilāyaṁ viharati niculavane.  
[an7.59:1.3] Atha kho āyasmā kimilo yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṁ abhivādetvā ekamantaṁ nisīdi. Ekamantaṁ nisinno kho āyasmā kimilo bhagavantaṁ etadavoca:  
[an7.59:1.4] “ko nu kho, bhante, hetu ko paccayo yena tathāgate parinibbute saddhammo na ciraṭṭhitiko hotī”ti?  
[an7.59:2.1] “Idha, kimila, tathāgate parinibbute bhikkhū bhikkhuniyo upāsakā upāsikāyo satthari agāravā viharanti appatissā, dhamme agāravā viharanti appatissā, saṅghe agāravā viharanti appatissā, sikkhāya agāravā viharanti appatissā, samādhismiṁ agāravā viharanti appatissā, appamāde agāravā viharanti appatissā, paṭisanthāre agāravā viharanti appatissā.  
[an7.59:2.2] Ayaṁ kho, kimila, hetu ayaṁ paccayo yena tathāgate parinibbute saddhammo na ciraṭṭhitiko hotī”ti.  
[an7.59:3.1] “Ko pana, bhante, hetu ko paccayo yena tathāgate parinibbute saddhammo ciraṭṭhitiko hotī”ti?  
[an7.59:3.2] “Idha, kimila, tathāgate parinibbute bhikkhū bhikkhuniyo upāsakā upāsikāyo satthari sagāravā viharanti sappatissā, dhamme sagāravā viharanti sappatissā, saṅghe sagāravā viharanti sappatissā, sikkhāya sagāravā viharanti sappatissā, samādhismiṁ sagāravā viharanti sappatissā, appamāde sagāravā viharanti sappatissā, paṭisanthāre sagāravā viharanti sappatissā.  
[an7.59:3.3] Ayaṁ kho, kimila, hetu ayaṁ paccayo yena tathāgate parinibbute saddhammo ciraṭṭhitiko hotī”ti.  
[an7.59:3.4] Chaṭṭhaṁ.

*DN 16:1.6–1.12* — Mahāparinibbānasutta

[dn16:1.6.0] 3. Bhikkhuaparihāniyadhamma  
[dn16:1.6.1] Atha kho bhagavā acirapakkante vassakāre brāhmaṇe magadhamahāmatte āyasmantaṁ ānandaṁ āmantesi:  
[dn16:1.6.2] “gaccha tvaṁ, ānanda, yāvatikā bhikkhū rājagahaṁ upanissāya viharanti, te sabbe upaṭṭhānasālāyaṁ sannipātehī”ti.  
[dn16:1.6.3] “Evaṁ, bhante”ti kho āyasmā ānando bhagavato paṭissutvā yāvatikā bhikkhū rājagahaṁ upanissāya viharanti, te sabbe upaṭṭhānasālāyaṁ sannipātetvā yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṁ abhivādetvā ekamantaṁ aṭṭhāsi. Ekamantaṁ ṭhito kho āyasmā ānando bhagavantaṁ etadavoca:  
[dn16:1.6.4] “sannipatito, bhante, bhikkhusaṅgho, yassadāni, bhante, bhagavā kālaṁ maññatī”ti.  
[dn16:1.6.5] Atha kho bhagavā uṭṭhāyāsanā yena upaṭṭhānasālā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā paññatte āsane nisīdi.  
[dn16:1.6.6] Nisajja kho bhagavā bhikkhū āmantesi:  
[dn16:1.6.7] “satta vo, bhikkhave, aparihāniye dhamme desessāmi,  
[dn16:1.6.8] taṁ suṇātha, sādhukaṁ manasikarotha, bhāsissāmī”ti.  
[dn16:1.6.9] “Evaṁ, bhante”ti kho te bhikkhū bhagavato paccassosuṁ.  
[dn16:1.6.10] Bhagavā etadavoca:  
[dn16:1.6.11] “Yāvakīvañca, bhikkhave, bhikkhū abhiṇhaṁ sannipātā sannipātabahulā bhavissanti, vuddhiyeva, bhikkhave, bhikkhūnaṁ pāṭikaṅkhā, no parihāni.  
[dn16:1.6.12] Yāvakīvañca, bhikkhave, bhikkhū samaggā sannipatissanti, samaggā vuṭṭhahissanti, samaggā saṅghakaraṇīyāni karissanti, vuddhiyeva, bhikkhave, bhikkhūnaṁ pāṭikaṅkhā, no parihāni.  
[dn16:1.6.13] Yāvakīvañca, bhikkhave, bhikkhū apaññattaṁ na paññapessanti, paññattaṁ na samucchindissanti, yathāpaññattesu sikkhāpadesu samādāya vattissanti, vuddhiyeva, bhikkhave, bhikkhūnaṁ pāṭikaṅkhā, no parihāni.  
[dn16:1.6.14] Yāvakīvañca, bhikkhave, bhikkhū ye te bhikkhū therā rattaññū cirapabbajitā saṅghapitaro saṅghapariṇāyakā, te sakkarissanti garuṁ karissanti mānessanti pūjessanti, tesañca sotabbaṁ maññissanti, vuddhiyeva, bhikkhave, bhikkhūnaṁ pāṭikaṅkhā, no parihāni.  
[dn16:1.6.15] Yāvakīvañca, bhikkhave, bhikkhū uppannāya taṇhāya ponobbhavikāya na vasaṁ gacchissanti, vuddhiyeva, bhikkhave, bhikkhūnaṁ pāṭikaṅkhā, no parihāni.  
[dn16:1.6.16] Yāvakīvañca, bhikkhave, bhikkhū āraññakesu senāsanesu sāpekkhā bhavissanti, vuddhiyeva, bhikkhave, bhikkhūnaṁ pāṭikaṅkhā, no parihāni.  
[dn16:1.6.17] Yāvakīvañca, bhikkhave, bhikkhū paccattaññeva satiṁ upaṭṭhapessanti: ‘kinti anāgatā ca pesalā sabrahmacārī āgaccheyyuṁ, āgatā ca pesalā sabrahmacārī phāsu vihareyyun’ti. Vuddhiyeva, bhikkhave, bhikkhūnaṁ pāṭikaṅkhā, no parihāni.  
[dn16:1.6.18] Yāvakīvañca, bhikkhave, ime satta aparihāniyā dhammā bhikkhūsu ṭhassanti, imesu ca sattasu aparihāniyesu dhammesu bhikkhū sandississanti, vuddhiyeva, bhikkhave, bhikkhūnaṁ pāṭikaṅkhā, no parihāni.

[dn16:1.7.5] Yāvakīvañca, bhikkhave, bhikkhū na bhassārāmā bhavissanti na bhassaratā na bhassārāmatamanuyuttā, vuddhiyeva, bhikkhave, bhikkhūnaṁ pāṭikaṅkhā, no parihāni.  
[dn16:1.7.6] Yāvakīvañca, bhikkhave, bhikkhū na niddārāmā bhavissanti na niddāratā na niddārāmatamanuyuttā, vuddhiyeva, bhikkhave, bhikkhūnaṁ pāṭikaṅkhā, no parihāni.
