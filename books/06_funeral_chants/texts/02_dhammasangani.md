**Kusalā dhammā, akusalā dhammā, abyākatā dhammā.**

**Literal:** Wholesome phenomena, unwholesome phenomena, indeterminate phenomena.  
**Faithful:** Wholesome phenomena, unwholesome phenomena, indeterminate phenomena.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |

| **`dhammā`** | `(masc nom pl)` phenomena, things; **[√dhar + ma]** |
