**Sukhassa dātā medhāvī sukhaṁ so adhigacchati**

**Faithful:** the intelligent giver of happiness: he obtains happiness.

**Dīgh'āyu yasavā hoti yattha yatthūpapajjatī'ti**

**Literal:** long-lived, famous is wherever wherever he is reborn [end of quotation]  
**Faithful:** is long-lived and famous wherever he is reborn.
