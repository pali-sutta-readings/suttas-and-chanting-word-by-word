# Mahā-maṅgala-cakka-vāḷa

*The Greater Circle of Blessings*

**Siri-dhiti-mati-tejo-jayasiddhi-mahiddhi-mahāguṇā-parimita-puññādhikarassa**

**Literal:** Of the glory-resolve-wisdom-splendour-victory-and-success-great-power-great-virtues-immeasurable-merit-store [one],  
**Faithful:** Of the one who has glory, resolve, wisdom, splendour, victory and success, great power, great virtues and an immeasurable store of merit,  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`siri-dhiti-mati-tejo-jayasiddhi-mahiddhi-mahāguṇā-parimita-puññādhikarassa`** | `(masc gen sg comp)` of the one who has glory, resolve, wisdom, splendour, victory and success, great power, great virtues and an immeasurable store of merit; **[siri + dhiti + mati + tejo + jayasiddhi + mahiddhi + mahāguṇa + aparimita + puñña + adhikāra]** |
| ↳ **`siri`** | `(fem comp)` glory, splendour, fortune; **[siri, no root in DPD]** |
| ↳ **`dhiti`** | `(fem comp)` resolve, constancy, determination; **[√dhar > dhir + ti]** |
| ↳ **`mati`** | `(fem comp)` wisdom, intelligence; **[√man + ti]** |
| ↳ **`tejo`** | `(masc comp)` [*tejas* in its compound form *tejo-*] splendour, power; **[√tij > tej + as]** |
| ↳ **`jayasiddhi`** | `(fem comp)` victory and success; **[jaya + siddhi]** |
| ↳ **`mahiddhi`** | `(fem comp)` great power, great psychic power; **[mahā + iddhi]** |
| ↳ **`mahāguṇā`** | `(masc comp)` [*mahāguṇa*, its *-a* merged with the *a-* of *aparimita*] great virtues; **[mahā + guṇa]** |
| ↳ **`parimita`** | `(pp comp)` [*aparimita*, its *a-* merged into the preceding *-ā*] immeasurable, limitless; **[na > a + pari + √mā > mi + ta]** |
| ↳ **`puññ`** | `(nt comp)` merit; **[√puṇ + ya]** |
| ↳ **`ādhikarassa`** | `(masc gen sg)` [*adhikāra*, *a-* lengthened in sandhi, *-ā-* shortened; as a final member, "having"] store (of merit), accomplishment; resolve; **[adhi + √kar > kār + a]** |
