# About This Book {.unnumbered}

This book gives the Pāli text of the *Mahāsatipaṭṭhānasutta*, the Great Discourse on the Establishments of Mindfulness (Dīgha Nikāya 22), for novice bhikkhus who want to read it word by word. It is a study text, to be read, not chanted.

The discourse is divided into sentences, and long sentences into clauses. Each is followed by a literal English rendering that keeps the Pāli word order, a more natural English rendering, and a table that analyses every word: its grammar, its meaning, and how it is built from its root. Every compound is also broken into its members. A passage that the discourse repeats word for word is given its table once; later it keeps its two renderings and points back to the first. Where a passage repeats with a few words changed (the nine charnel grounds, the hindrances, the sense bases), the changed words follow it, each with a short table.

## Where the Pāli comes from {.unnumbered .unlisted}

- **SuttaCentral**, Mahāsaṅgīti edition of the Pāli Canon (suttacentral.net, text *dn22*). SuttaCentral states that the original Pāli texts are in the public domain. Its spelling and word-joining are kept. Its notes on variant readings are left out.
- Where SuttaCentral abbreviates a repeated passage (*…pe…*, “…”), it is written out in full here from the discourse's own wording.

The niggahīta is written ṁ throughout. The headings keep SuttaCentral's section numbers, and the citation under each section gives its SuttaCentral segment numbers (*DN 22:2.1–2.15*).

## How the English was made {.unnumbered .unlisted}

The renderings were made from the Pāli and checked against the Digital Pāḷi Dictionary. They are not taken from any published translation. Where a passage also appears in the chanting books of this series, its renderings and analysis are the ones given there. The first drafts of the renderings and word analyses were produced with an AI language model working from the Pāli text, following a fixed set of rules for format, grammar labels and roots.

## How the text was checked {.unnumbered .unlisted}

Each section was checked line by line before this book was built:

- the Pāli was compared with SuttaCentral's text, so that nothing is missing, added or changed apart from the abbreviations written out;
- every word's grammar, root and meaning was reviewed, with the Digital Pāḷi Dictionary (DPD, dpdict.net) as the reference for roots and analysis;
- the analyses were compared with those in the other books of this series, so that a word is analysed the same way wherever its grammar and meaning are the same;
- the files were checked for stray text, formatting errors and character-encoding problems.

The checking was carried out with the help of an AI language model (Claude, made by Anthropic), working to written rules, with every change recorded in a log. No independent Pāli scholar has reviewed the text.

This is a study aid. It is not a critical edition, and errors may remain.
