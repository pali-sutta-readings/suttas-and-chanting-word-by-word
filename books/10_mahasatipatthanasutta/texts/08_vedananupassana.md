# Vedanānupassanā

*Contemplation of Feelings*

### 2 Vedanānupassanā

*Contemplation of feelings*

**Kathañca pana, bhikkhave, bhikkhu vedanāsu vedanānupassī viharati?**

**Literal:** How-and then, bhikkhus, [does] a-bhikkhu in-feelings feeling-contemplating dwell?  
**Faithful:** And how, bhikkhus, does a bhikkhu dwell contemplating feelings as feelings?  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |

| **`bhikkhave`** | `(masc voc pl)` bhikkhus, monks; **[√bhikkh + u + ave]** |
