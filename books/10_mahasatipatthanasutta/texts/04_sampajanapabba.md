# Sampajānapabba

*Clear Comprehension*

### 1.3 Kāyānupassanāsampajānapabba

*Contemplation of the body: the section on clear comprehension*

**Puna caparaṁ, bhikkhave, bhikkhu abhikkante paṭikkante sampajānakārī hoti,**

**Literal:** Again and-further, bhikkhus, a-bhikkhu in-going-forward in-returning clearly-comprehending-acting is;  
**Faithful:** Again, bhikkhus, a bhikkhu acts with clear comprehension in going forward and in returning;

**Iti ajjhattaṁ vā kāye kāyānupassī viharati,**

**Literal:** Thus internally or in-the-body body-contemplating he-dwells,  
**Faithful:** In this way he dwells contemplating the body as a body internally,  

*Word analysis as at the first occurrence of this line.*

**bahiddhā vā kāye kāyānupassī viharati,**

**Literal:** externally or in-the-body body-contemplating he-dwells,  
**Faithful:** or he dwells contemplating the body as a body externally,  

*Word analysis as at the first occurrence of this line.*

**ajjhattabahiddhā vā kāye kāyānupassī viharati.**

**Literal:** internally-and-externally or in-the-body body-contemplating he-dwells.  
**Faithful:** or he dwells contemplating the body as a body both internally and externally.  

*Word analysis as at the first occurrence of this line.*

**Samudayadhammānupassī vā kāyasmiṁ viharati,**

**Faithful:** or he dwells contemplating the nature of arising and vanishing in the body.  

*Word analysis as at the first occurrence of this line.*

**Atthi kāyo'ti vā panassa sati paccupaṭṭhitā hoti yāvadeva ñāṇamattāya paṭissatimattāya**

**Literal:** "There is [a] body" thus or, moreover for-him mindfulness present is, only-as-far-as for-the-measure-of-knowing, for-the-measure-of-mindfulness,  
**Faithful:** Or else mindfulness that "there is a body" is present in him only to the extent of bare knowing and continuous mindfulness,  

*Word analysis as at the first occurrence of this line.*

**anissito ca viharati,**

**Literal:** independent and he-dwells,  
**Faithful:** and he dwells independent,  

*Word analysis as at the first occurrence of this line.*

**na ca kiñci loke upādiyati.**

**Literal:** not and anything in-the-world he-clings-to.  
**Faithful:** and he does not cling to anything in the world.  

*Word analysis as at the first occurrence of this line.*

**Evampi kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhu kāye kāyānupassī viharati.**

**Literal:** In-this-way-too indeed, bhikkhus, a-bhikkhu in-the-body body-contemplating dwells.  
**Faithful:** It is in this way too, bhikkhus, that a bhikkhu dwells contemplating the body as a body.  

*Word analysis as at the first occurrence of this line.*
