# Navasivathikapabba

*The Nine Charnel Grounds*

### 1.6 Kāyānupassanānavasivathikapabba

**Puna caparaṁ, bhikkhave, bhikkhu seyyathāpi passeyya sarīraṁ sivathikāya chaḍḍitaṁ**

**Literal:** Again and-further, bhikkhus, a-bhikkhu, just-as-if he-would-see [a] body in-a-charnel-ground thrown-away,
