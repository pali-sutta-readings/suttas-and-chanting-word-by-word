# Saṅghābhigīti

*Supreme Praise of the Saṅgha*

**Handa mayaṁ saṅghābhigītiṁ karomase**

**Literal:** Come now, we [the] high song of praise of [the] Saṅgha let us make  
**Faithful:** Now let us chant the high praise of the Saṅgha.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`handa`** | `(interj)` come now, well then; **[handa]** |
| **`mayaṁ`** | `(1st pl nom)` we; **[ahaṁ]** |
| **`saṅghābhigītiṁ`** | `(fem acc sg comp)` high song of praise of the Saṅgha; **[saṅgha + abhigīti]** |
| ↳ **`saṅgha`** | `(masc comp)` Saṅgha; **[saṁ + √ghaṭ + a]** |
| ↳ **`abhigītiṁ`** | `(fem acc sg)` high song, song of praise; **[abhi + √gā + ti]** |
| **`karomase`** | `(1st pl pres mid imperat)` let us make, let us do; **[√kar + o + mase]** |
