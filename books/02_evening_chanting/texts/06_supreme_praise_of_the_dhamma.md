# Dhammābhigīti

*Supreme Praise of the Dhamma*

**Handa mayaṁ dhammābhigītiṁ karomase**

**Literal:** Come now, we [the] high song of praise of [the] Dhamma let us make  
**Faithful:** Now let us chant the high praise of the Dhamma.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`handa`** | `(interj)` come now, well then; **[handa]** |
| **`mayaṁ`** | `(1st pl nom)` we; **[ahaṁ]** |
| **`dhammābhigītiṁ`** | `(fem acc sg comp)` high song of praise of the Dhamma; **[dhamma + abhigīti]** |
| ↳ **`dhamma`** | `(masc comp)` Dhamma; **[√dhar + ma]** |

| **`karomase`** | `(1st pl pres mid imperat)` let us make, let us do; **[√kar + o + mase]** |
