# Sakkatvā buddharatanaṁ

*Having Honoured the Jewel of the Buddha*

**Sakkatvā buddharatanaṁ**

**Literal:** Having honoured [the] Buddha-jewel,  
**Faithful:** Having honoured the jewel of the Buddha,  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`sakkatvā`** | `(abs)` having honoured, having revered; **[sat + √kar + tvā]** |
| **`buddharatanaṁ`** | `(nt acc sg comp)` jewel of the Buddha; **[buddha + ratana]** |
| ↳ **`buddha`** | `(masc comp)` Buddha; **[√budh + ta]** |
| ↳ **`ratanaṁ`** | `(nt acc sg)` jewel, treasure; **[ratana, no root in DPD]** |
