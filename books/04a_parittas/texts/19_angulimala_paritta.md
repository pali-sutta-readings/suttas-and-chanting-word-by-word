# Aṅgulimāla-paritta

*Aṅgulimāla's Protection*

**Yato'haṁ bhagini ariyāya jātiyā jāto**

**Literal:** Since I, sister, in [the] noble birth [was] born,  
**Faithful:** Sister, since I was born with the noble birth,  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`yato'haṁ`** | `(sandhi)` [*yato* (ind) + *ahaṁ* (1st sg nom)] since I; **[yato (ya + to) + ahaṁ]** |
| **`bhagini`** | `(fem voc sg)` sister; **[√bhaj > bhag + a + inī]** |
| **`ariyāya`** | `(adj fem instr/loc sg)` noble; **[ariya, no root in DPD]** |
| **`jātiyā`** | `(fem instr/loc sg)` birth; **[√jan > jā + ti]** |
| **`jāto`** | `(pp masc nom sg)` born; **[√jan > jā + ta]** |

**Nābhijānāmi sañcicca pāṇaṁ jīvitā voropetā**

**Literal:** I do not recall intentionally a living being of life [being] a depriver.  
**Faithful:** I do not recall intentionally depriving a living being of life.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`nābhijānāmi`** | `(1st sg pres act indic)` [*na* + *abhijānāmi*] I do not recall, I am not aware; **[na + abhi + √ñā + nā + mi]** |
| **`sañcicca`** | `(ind)` intentionally, deliberately; **[saṁ + √cit + ya]** |
| **`pāṇaṁ`** | `(masc acc sg)` living being, breathing thing; **[pa + √an + a]** |
| **`jīvitā`** | `(nt abl sg)` from life; **[√jīv + ita]** |
| **`voropetā`** | `(masc nom sg)` [agent noun] one who deprives (someone of life); **[vi + ava + √ruh + e + tar]** |

**Tena saccena sotthi te hotu sotthi gabbhassa (×3)**

**Literal:** By that truth safety for you may be, safety for [the] womb.  
**Faithful:** By this truth, may you be safe, and may the child in your womb be safe.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`tena`** | `(dem pron nt instr sg)` by that; **[ta]** |
| **`saccena`** | `(nt instr sg)` by the truth; **[sat + ya (√as)]** |
| **`sotthi`** | `(fem nom sg)` safety, well-being; **[su > so + √as + ti]** |
| **`te`** | `(2nd sg dat)` for you, to you; **[tvaṁ]** |
| **`hotu`** | `(3rd sg pres act imperat)` may it be, may there be; **[√hū > ho + tu]** |
| **`sotthi`** | `(fem nom sg)` safety, well-being; **[su > so + √as + ti]** |
| **`gabbhassa`** | `(masc dat sg)` for the womb, for the child in the womb; **[gabbha, no root in DPD]** |
