# Bhavatu sabba-maṅgalaṁ

*May There Be Every Blessing*

**Bhavatu sabba-maṅgalaṁ rakkhantu sabba-devatā**

**Literal:** May be every-blessing; may protect [you] all-devas.  
**Faithful:** May there be every blessing; may all the devas protect you.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`bhavatu`** | `(3rd sg pres act imperat)` may there be; **[√bhū + a + tu]** |
| **`sabba-maṅgalaṁ`** | `(nt nom sg comp)` every blessing; **[sabba + maṅgala]** |
| ↳ **`sabba`** | `(pron comp)` all, every; **[sabba]** |
| ↳ **`maṅgalaṁ`** | `(nt nom sg)` blessing, auspicious thing; **[√maṅg + ala]** |
| **`rakkhantu`** | `(3rd pl pres act imperat)` may they protect; **[√rakkh + a + ntu]** |
| **`sabba-devatā`** | `(fem nom pl comp)` all the devas; **[sabba + devatā]** |
| ↳ **`sabba`** | `(pron comp)` all; **[sabba]** |
| ↳ **`devatā`** | `(fem nom pl)` devas, deities; **[√div > dev + a + tā]** |

**Sabba-buddhānubhāvena sadā sotthī bhavantu te**

**Literal:** By all-Buddhas-power always safeties may be for you.  
**Faithful:** By the power of all the Buddhas, may you always be safe.
