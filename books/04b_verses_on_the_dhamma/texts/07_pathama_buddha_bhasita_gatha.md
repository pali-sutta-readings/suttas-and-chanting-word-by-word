# Paṭhama-buddha-bhāsita-gāthā

*Verses of the Buddha's First Utterance*

**Aneka-jāti-saṁsāraṁ**

**Literal:** Many-birth-wandering  
**Faithful:** Through a round of many births  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`aneka-jāti-saṁsāraṁ`** | `(masc acc sg comp)` [accusative of duration] through a wandering on of many births; **[aneka + jāti + saṁsāra]** |
| ↳ **`aneka`** | `(adj comp)` many, numerous; **[na > an + eka]** |
| ↳ **`jāti`** | `(fem comp)` birth; **[√jan > jā + ti]** |
| ↳ **`saṁsāraṁ`** | `(masc acc sg)` wandering on, the round of rebirth; **[saṁ + √sar > sār + a]** |

**sandhāvissaṁ anibbisaṁ**

**Literal:** I-wandered not-finding,  
**Faithful:** I wandered on without finding,  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`sandhāvissaṁ`** | `(1st sg aor act indic)` I wandered, I ran on; **[saṁ + √dhāv + issaṁ]** |
| **`anibbisaṁ`** | `(prp masc nom sg)` not finding, not getting; **[na > a + nī + √vis + a + nta]** |

| **`gavesanto`** | `(prp masc nom sg)` seeking, searching for; **[√gaves + a + nta]** |

**dukkhā jāti punappunaṁ**

**Literal:** Painful [is] birth again-and-again.  
**Faithful:** Painful is birth again and again.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |

| **`punappunaṁ`** | `(ind)` again and again, repeatedly; **[puna + puna + aṁ]** |

**Literal:** again house not you-will-make.

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`puna`** | `(ind)` again; **[puna]** |

| **`na`** | `(ind)` not; **[na]** |
| **`kāhasi`** | `(2nd sg fut act indic)` you will make, you will build; **[√kar > kāha + si]** |

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
