# Nakkhattayakkha

*Closing Verse against Malign Stars and Spirits*

**Nakkhatta-yakkha-bhūtānaṁ**

**Literal:** Of-stars-yakkhas-beings,  
**Faithful:** Of stars, yakkhas and spirits,  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`nakkhatta-yakkha-bhūtānaṁ`** | `(nt gen pl comp)` of stars, yakkhas and beings; **[nakkhatta + yakkha + bhūta]** |
| ↳ **`nakkhatta`** | `(nt comp)` star, constellation; **[√nakkh + a + tta]** |
| ↳ **`yakkha`** | `(masc comp)` yakkha, spirit; **[√yakkh + a]** |
| ↳ **`bhūtānaṁ`** | `(nt gen pl)` of beings, of spirits; **[√bhū + ta]** |

**Pāpa-ggaha-nivāraṇā**

**Literal:** through-evil-planet-warding-off,  
**Faithful:** through the warding off of evil planets,  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`pāpa-ggaha-nivāraṇā`** | `(nt abl sg comp)` [ablative of means] through warding off evil planets; **[pāpa + gaha + nivāraṇa]** |
| ↳ **`pāpa`** | `(adj comp)` evil, bad; **[pāpa, no root in DPD]** |
| ↳ **`ggaha`** | `(masc comp)` [*gaha* with *g-* doubled in the compound] planet; **[√gah + a]** |
| ↳ **`nivāraṇā`** | `(nt abl sg)` through warding off, by preventing; **[ni + √var + e + aṇa]** |

**Parittassānubhāvena**

**Literal:** by-the-paritta's-power,  
**Faithful:** by the power of the paritta,  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`parittassānubhāvena`** | `(sandhi)` [*parittassa* (nt gen sg) + *ānubhāvena* (masc instr sg)] by the power of the paritta; **[pari + √tā + a; + anu > ānu + √bhū > bhāv + a + a]** |

**Hantvā tesaṁ upaddave (×3)**

**Literal:** having-struck-down their calamities.  
**Faithful:** may their calamities be struck down.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`hantvā`** | `(abs)` [absolutive with no main verb, read here as a wish] having struck down, having destroyed; **[√han + tvā]** |
| **`tesaṁ`** | `(dem pron nt gen pl)` their, of those; **[ta]** |
| **`upaddave`** | `(masc acc pl)` calamities, dangers; **[upa + √du > dav + a]** |
