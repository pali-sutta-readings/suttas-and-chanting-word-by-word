# Ratanattaya-paṇāma-gāthā

**Literal:** Come now, we Triple-Gem-salutation verses and also urgency-proclaiming passage let us recite

| ↳ **`taya`** | `(nt comp)` triad, group of three; **[ti + a]** |

| ↳ **`gāthāyo`** | `(fem acc pl)` verses; **[√gā + thā]** |
