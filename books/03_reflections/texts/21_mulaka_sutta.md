# Mūlaka-sutta-pāṭha

**Literal:** Come now, we [the] roots-discourse-passage let us recite  
**Faithful:** Come now, let us recite the passage from the Discourse on Roots.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |

| ↳ **`mūlaka`** | `(adj comp)` having roots, rooted; **[√mūl + a + ka]** |
| ↳ **`sutta`** | `(nt comp)` discourse; **[√siv > suv + ta]** |
| ↳ **`pāṭhaṁ`** | `(masc acc sg)` passage, text for recitation; **[√paṭh > pāṭh + a]** |
