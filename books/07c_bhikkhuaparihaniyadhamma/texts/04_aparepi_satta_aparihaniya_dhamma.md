# Aparepi Satta Aparihāniyā Dhammā

*Seven Further Qualities That Prevent Decline: The Fourth Set*

**Aparepi vo, bhikkhave, satta aparihāniye dhamme desessāmi,**

**Literal:** Further too to you, bhikkhus, seven non-decline qualities I will teach;  
**Faithful:** Bhikkhus, I will teach you seven further qualities that do not lead to decline.
