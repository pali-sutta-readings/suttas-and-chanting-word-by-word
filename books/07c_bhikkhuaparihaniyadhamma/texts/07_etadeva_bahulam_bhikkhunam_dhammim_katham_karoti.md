# Etadeva Bahulaṁ Bhikkhūnaṁ Dhammiṁ Kathaṁ Karoti

*The Dhamma Talk the Blessed One Often Gave*

**Tatra sudaṁ bhagavā rājagahe viharanto gijjhakūṭe pabbate**

**Literal:** There indeed the Blessed One, at Rājagaha dwelling, on Vulture's Peak mountain,  
**Faithful:** There indeed the Blessed One, while dwelling at Rājagaha on the mountain of Vulture's Peak,

| :--- | :--- |

| ↳ **`gahe`** | `(nt loc sg)` house; **[√gah + a]** |
