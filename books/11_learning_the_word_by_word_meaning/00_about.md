# About This Book {.unnumbered}

This book is a short guide to learning the word-by-word meaning of the Pāli chanting in this series. It explains how to use the translations, how to make a Leitner box for reviewing vocabulary, and how to make and review flashcards for words, phrases and grammar.

## Where the text comes from {.unnumbered .unlisted}

The text is the author's guide, reproduced from the original Word document with light copy-editing. The example is the first line of the Yathā vāri-vahā pūrā passage in *Anumodanā* (book 05), and its word table is the one printed there.

The diagrams are the original screenshots of hand-made flashcards.

## How the text was prepared {.unnumbered .unlisted}

The Word document was converted to Markdown, then checked against the original paragraph by paragraph. Headings were arranged for this edition, the word table was replaced with the checked table from book 05, the links were given full addresses in the Resources section, and the screenshots were placed as figures. Obvious slips of spelling and punctuation were corrected; the wording is otherwise the author's.
