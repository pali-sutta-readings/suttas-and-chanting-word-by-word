# About This Book {.unnumbered}

This book gives the Pāli text of the suttas (Sutta-pāṭha), as chanted in monasteries of the Ajahn Chah lineage in the West. Each line of Pāli is followed by a literal English rendering that keeps the Pāli word order, a more natural English rendering, and a table that analyses every word: its grammar, its meaning, and how it is built from its root.

## Where the Pāli comes from {.unnumbered .unlisted}

- **Bhikkhu Manual**, Reference Edition (Amaravati Publications, 2020; CC BY-NC-ND 4.0) is the main source. Its spelling and word-joining are kept, with these changes: the leader's opening words, printed in brackets there, are not marked here; a few obvious misprints are corrected; and the few words it leaves out where the other two sources agree are restored.
- **Chanting Guide** (Dhammayut Order in the United States of America, fourth edition 2017; CC BY-NC 4.0) is used for chants that are not in the Bhikkhu Manual.
- **SuttaCentral** (Mahāsaṅgīti edition of the Pāli Canon; CC0) is used for chants found in neither book, and as a cross-check.

The niggahīta is written ṁ throughout. Citations under each passage are SuttaCentral text IDs; commentaries, which SuttaCentral does not host, keep the Bhikkhu Manual's references.

## How the English was made {.unnumbered .unlisted}

The first drafts of the renderings and word analyses were produced with an AI language model working from the Pāli text, following a fixed set of rules for format, grammar labels and roots.

The English renderings were made from the Pāli. They are not taken from the translations printed in the source books.

## How the text was checked {.unnumbered .unlisted}

Each chant was checked line by line before this book was built:

- the Pāli was compared with the source text, so that nothing is missing, added or changed;
- every word's grammar, root and meaning was reviewed, with the Digital Pāḷi Dictionary (DPD integrated into the [Simsapa CLI](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@simsapa/cli) gloss tooling) as the reference for roots and analysis; traditional explanations that are not historical derivations are marked “(trad.)”;
- the analyses were compared across all the books in this series, and a word is analysed the same way wherever its grammar and meaning are the same (a few differences of notation remain);
- the files were checked for stray text, formatting errors and character-encoding problems.

The checking was carried out with the help of an AI language model (Claude, made by Anthropic), working to written rules, with every change recorded in a log. No independent Pāli scholar has reviewed the text.

This is a study aid for chanting with understanding. It is not a critical edition, and errors may remain.
