# Anatta-lakkhaṇa-sutta

*The Discourse on the Characteristic of Not-self*

*(Solo introduction)*

**Yantaṁ sattehi dukkhena ñeyyaṁ anattalakkhaṇaṁ**

**Literal:** Which-that by beings with difficulty to be known, the not-self characteristic,  
**Faithful:** That characteristic of not-self, hard for beings to know,  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`yantaṁ`** | `(sandhi)` [*yaṁ* (rel pron nt nom sg) + *taṁ* (dem pron nt nom sg)] which, that; **[ya; ta]** |
| **`sattehi`** | `(masc instr pl)` by beings, by living beings; **[√as > sa + tta]** |
| **`dukkhena`** | `(nt instr sg)` [used adverbially] with difficulty, hardly; **[√dukkh + a]** |
| **`ñeyyaṁ`** | `(ptp nt nom sg)` to be known, to be understood; **[√ñā + eyya]** |
| **`anattalakkhaṇaṁ`** | `(nt nom sg comp)` the characteristic of not-self; **[anatta + lakkhaṇa]** |
| ↳ **`anatta`** | `(masc comp)` not-self; **[na > an + atta]** |
| ↳ **`lakkhaṇaṁ`** | `(nt nom sg)` characteristic, mark; **[√lakkh + aṇa]** |

**Faithful:** the minds of the bhikkhus of the group of five were released from the taints through not clinging.

| **`anupādāya`** | `(abs)` without clinging, not grasping; **[na > an + upa + √ādā + ya]** |

**Anattalakkhaṇa-suttaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.**

**Literal:** The not-self-characteristic discourse [is] finished.  
**Faithful:** The Discourse on the Characteristic of Not-self is finished.
