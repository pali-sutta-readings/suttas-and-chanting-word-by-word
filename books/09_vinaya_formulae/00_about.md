# About This Book {.unnumbered}

This book gives the Pāli formulae of the Vinaya sections of the *Bhikkhu Manual*: for requisites, the Rains and the Kaṭhina, other procedures, offences, and the Uposatha. It is for bhikkhus who already use these formulae and want to understand each word. The *Bhikkhu Manual* remains the guide to the procedures: its rules and explanations are not repeated here, only enough to say who speaks, to whom, and when.

Each formula is followed by a literal English rendering that keeps the Pāli word order, the *Bhikkhu Manual*'s own English rendering, and a table that analyses every word: its grammar, its meaning, and how it is built from its root. Where a formula changes with the situation (a senior or junior listener, one article or several, near or far), the changed words follow it, each with a short table.

## Where the text comes from {.unnumbered .unlisted}

- **Bhikkhu Manual**, Reference Edition (Amaravati Publications, 2020; CC BY-NC-ND 4.0) is the only source of the Pāli and of the renderings marked “Faithful”. Its spelling and word-joining are kept, with these changes: a few obvious misprints are corrected; formulae it abbreviates are given in full, from its own wording; and the leader's words, printed in brackets there, are not marked here.
- Where a formula also appears in the chanting books of this series, its analysis is the one given there.

The niggahīta is written ṁ throughout. Citations under each formula are SuttaCentral text IDs; commentaries, which SuttaCentral does not host, keep the *Bhikkhu Manual*'s references (*Sp* = Samantapāsādikā, *Kv* = Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī).

## How the English was made {.unnumbered .unlisted}

The “Faithful” lines are the *Bhikkhu Manual*'s English. Where it abbreviates (“… these robes …”), the line is completed from its own wording. Where it gives no English, a new rendering was made from the Pāli. The literal lines and the word tables are new. The first drafts of the renderings and word analyses were produced with an AI language model working from the Pāli text, following a fixed set of rules for format, grammar labels and roots.

## How the text was checked {.unnumbered .unlisted}

Each formula was checked line by line before this book was built:

- the Pāli was compared with the *Bhikkhu Manual*, so that nothing is missing, added or changed apart from the corrections listed in the change log;
- every word's grammar, root and meaning was reviewed, with the Digital Pāḷi Dictionary (DPD, dpdict.net) as the reference for roots and analysis;
- the analyses were compared with those in the chanting books of this series, so that a word is analysed the same way wherever its grammar and meaning are the same;
- the files were checked for stray text, formatting errors and character-encoding problems.

The checking was carried out with the help of an AI language model (Claude, made by Anthropic), working to written rules, with every change recorded in a log. No independent Pāli scholar has reviewed the text.

This is a study aid. It is not a critical edition, and errors may remain.
