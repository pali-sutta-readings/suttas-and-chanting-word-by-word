# Pavāraṇā

*Inviting Admonition: a Sick Bhikkhu*

> The sick bhikkhu, to the bhikkhu who will convey his invitation:

**Pavāraṇaṁ dammi, pavāraṇaṁ me hara,**

**Literal:** [My] invitation I give, [the] invitation for me carry,  
**Faithful:** I give my invitation. May you convey invitation for me.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`pavāraṇaṁ`** | `(fem acc sg)` invitation (to admonition), Pavāraṇā; **[pa + √var + e + anā]** |
| **`dammi`** | `(1st sg pres act indic)` I give; **[√dā + āmi > dammi]** |
| **`pavāraṇaṁ`** | `(fem acc sg)` invitation (to admonition), Pavāraṇā; **[pa + √var + e + anā]** |
| **`me`** | `(1st sg dat)` for me, on my behalf; **[ahaṁ]** |
| **`hara`** | `(2nd sg pres act imperat)` carry, convey, take; **[√har + a]** |

**mam'atthāya pavārehi.**

**Literal:** for my sake invite.  
**Faithful:** May you invite on my behalf.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`mam'atthāya`** | `(sandhi)` [*mama* (1st sg gen) my + *atthāya* (masc dat sg) for the sake] for my sake, on my behalf; **[ahaṁ; √ar + tha + āya]** |
| **`pavārehi`** | `(2nd sg pres act imperat)` invite, make the Pavāraṇā; **[pa + √var + e + hi]** |

**Variant:** the sick bhikkhu is the junior one: *hara* → *haratha*; *pavārehi* → *pavāretha*  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`haratha`** | `(2nd pl pres act imperat)` [plural of respect] carry, convey, take; **[√har + a + tha]** |
| **`pavāretha`** | `(2nd pl pres act imperat)` [plural of respect] invite, make the Pavāraṇā; **[pa + √var + e + tha]** |

> The sick bhikkhu's invitation (e.g. Uttaro) is conveyed in his place in the order of Rains:

**Diṭṭhena vā sutena vā parisaṅkāya vā, vadantu taṁ āyasmanto anukampaṁ upādāya.**

**Literal:** By [what is] seen or by [what is] heard or by suspicion or, may speak [to] him [the] venerable ones, compassion taking up.  
**Faithful:** With what you have seen, heard and suspected, may all of you instruct him out of compassion.
