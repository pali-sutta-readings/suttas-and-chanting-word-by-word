# Adhiṭṭhānuposatha

*For a Lone Bhikkhu*

> For a bhikkhu staying alone on the Uposatha day. After the preliminary duties, he then determines:

**Ajja me uposatho.**

**Literal:** Today for me [is the] Uposatha.  
**Faithful:** Today is an Observance day for me.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`ajja`** | `(ind)` today; **[ima + jja]** |
| **`me`** | `(1st sg dat)` for me; **[ahaṁ]** |
| **`uposatho`** | `(masc nom sg)` Uposatha, observance day; **[upa + √vas + a + tha]** |
