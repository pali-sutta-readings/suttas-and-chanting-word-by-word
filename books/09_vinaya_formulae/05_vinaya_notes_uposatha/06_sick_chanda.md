# Chanda

*Sick Bhikkhus: Sending Consent*

> The sick bhikkhu sends his consent to the *saṅghakamma*:

**Chandaṁ dammi,**

**Literal:** [My] consent [I] give,  
**Faithful:** I offer my consent.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`chandaṁ`** | `(masc acc sg)` (vinaya) consent, approval; **[√chand + a]** |
| **`dammi`** | `(1st sg pres act indic)` I give; **[√dā + āmi > dammi]** |

**chandaṁ me hara,**

**Literal:** [the] consent for me carry,  
**Faithful:** May you convey my consent (to the Saṅgha).  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`chandaṁ`** | `(masc acc sg)` (vinaya) consent, approval; **[√chand + a]** |
| **`me`** | `(1st sg dat)` for me, on my behalf; **[ahaṁ]** |
| **`hara`** | `(2nd sg pres act imperat)` carry, convey, take; **[√har + a]** |

**chandaṁ me ārocehi.**

**Literal:** [the] consent for me announce.  
**Faithful:** May you declare my consent to them.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`chandaṁ`** | `(masc acc sg)` (vinaya) consent, approval; **[√chand + a]** |
| **`me`** | `(1st sg dat)` for me, on my behalf; **[ahaṁ]** |
| **`ārocehi`** | `(2nd sg pres caus act imperat)` announce, declare, inform (the Saṅgha); **[ā + √ruc > roce + hi]** |

**Variant:** the sick bhikkhu is the junior: *hara* → *haratha*; *ārocehi* → *ārocetha*  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`haratha`** | `(2nd pl pres act imperat)` [plural of respect] carry, convey, take; **[√har + a + tha]** |
| **`ārocetha`** | `(2nd pl pres caus act imperat)` [plural of respect] announce, declare, inform; **[ā + √ruc > roce + tha]** |

> Informing the Saṅgha of the sick bhikkhu's consent:

**Āyasmā bhante uttaro mayhaṁ chandaṁ adāsi,**

**Literal:** [The] venerable, venerable sirs, Uttaro to me [his] consent gave;  
**Faithful:** Ven. sirs, Uttaro Bhikkhu has given his consent to me.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`āyasmā`** | `(masc nom sg)` the venerable; **[ā + √i > ay + as + mant]** |
| **`bhante`** | `(masc voc pl)` venerable sirs; **[√bhū > bhava + nta + e]** |
| **`uttaro`** | `(masc nom sg)` [example name; substitute the actual name] Uttaro; **[proper name]** |
| **`mayhaṁ`** | `(1st sg dat)` to me; **[ahaṁ]** |
| **`chandaṁ`** | `(masc acc sg)` (vinaya) consent, approval; **[√chand + a]** |
| **`adāsi`** | `(3rd sg aor act indic)` gave, has given; **[a + √dā + si]** |

**tassa chando mayā āhaṭo,**

**Literal:** his consent by me [has been] brought;  
**Faithful:** I have conveyed his consent.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |

| **`mayā`** | `(1st sg instr)` by me; **[ahaṁ]** |

**sādhu bhante saṅgho dhāretu.**

**Literal:** good, venerable sirs, [the] Community may consider [it so].  
**Faithful:** It is well, ven. sirs, if the Saṅgha holds it to be so.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`sādhu`** | `(ind)` it is good, well; **[√sādh + u]** |
| **`bhante`** | `(masc voc pl)` venerable sirs; **[√bhū > bhava + nta + e]** |
| **`saṅgho`** | `(masc nom sg)` Community, Saṅgha; **[saṁ + √ghaṭ + a]** |
| **`dhāretu`** | `(3rd sg pres act imperat)` may (it) consider, may (it) hold (it so), may (it) accept; **[√dhar + e > dhāre + tu]** |

**Variant:** the conveying bhikkhu is senior to the sick bhikkhu: *Āyasmā bhante uttaro* → *Uttaro bhante bhikkhu*  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`uttaro`** | `(masc nom sg)` [example name; substitute the actual name] Uttaro; **[proper name]** |
| **`bhante`** | `(masc voc pl)` venerable sirs; **[√bhū > bhava + nta + e]** |
| **`bhikkhu`** | `(masc nom sg)` [in apposition to *uttaro*] the bhikkhu, the monk; **[√bhikkh + u]** |
