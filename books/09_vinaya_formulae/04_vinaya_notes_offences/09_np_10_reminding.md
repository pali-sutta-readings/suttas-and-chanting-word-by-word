# Nissaggiya Pācittiya 10

*Reminding*

**Idaṁ me bhante cīvaraṁ atireka-tikkhattuṁ codanāya atireka-chakkhattuṁ ṭhānena abhinipphāditaṁ nissaggiyaṁ, imāhaṁ āyasmato nissajjāmi.**

**Literal:** This by me, venerable sir, robe, more than three times by prompting, more than six times by standing, obtained, [is] to be forfeited; this I to [the] venerable [one] forfeit.  
**Faithful:** This robe, ven. sir, which has been obtained by inciting more than three times, by standing more than six times, is to be forfeited by me: I forfeit it to you.

> Returning the robe:

**Imaṁ cīvaraṁ āyasmato dammi.**

**Literal:** This robe to [the] venerable [one] [I] give.  
**Faithful:** I give this robe to you.
