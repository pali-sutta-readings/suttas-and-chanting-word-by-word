# Nissaggiya Pācittiya 30

*Diverted Gain*

**Idaṁ me bhante jānaṁ saṅghikaṁ lābhaṁ pariṇataṁ attano pariṇāmitaṁ nissaggiyaṁ. Imāhaṁ āyasmato nissajjāmi.**

**Literal:** This by me, venerable sir, knowingly, [a] Community-belonging gain designated [for another], to myself diverted, [is] to be forfeited. This I to [the] venerable [one] forfeit.  
**Faithful:** This gain belonging to the Saṅgha, ven. sir, which has been (already) diverted (to someone), (and) which has been knowingly diverted to myself (instead), is to be forfeited by me: I forfeit it to you.

> To return the article:

**Imaṁ āyasmato dammi.**

**Literal:** This to [the] venerable [one] [I] give.  
**Faithful:** I give this to you.
