# Pārisuddhi-uposatha

*Purity Uposatha: Before the Saṅgha*

> Declaring one's purity before the Saṅgha:

**Parisuddho ahaṁ bhante, parisuddho'ti maṁ saṅgho dhāretu.**

**Literal:** Pure [am] I, venerable sirs; as pure me [the] Community may consider.  
**Faithful:** I, ven. sirs, am quite pure (of offences). May the Saṅgha hold me to be pure.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`parisuddho`** | `(pp masc nom sg)` pure, (quite) pure (of offences); **[pari + √sudh + ta]** |
| **`ahaṁ`** | `(1st sg nom)` I; **[ahaṁ]** |

| **`saṅgho`** | `(masc nom sg)` Community, Saṅgha; **[saṁ + √ghaṭ + a]** |
