# Pārisuddhi and Chanda

*Sick Bhikkhus: Purity and Consent*

> When both purity and consent are conveyed to the Sangha:

**Uttaro bhante bhikkhu gilāno**

**Literal:** Uttaro, venerable sirs, [the] bhikkhu, sick,  
**Faithful:** Ven. sirs, Uttaro Bhikkhu is sick.

**sādhu bhante saṅgho dhāretu.**

**Literal:** good, venerable sirs, [the] Community may consider [it so].  
**Faithful:** It is well, ven. sirs, if the Sangha holds it to be so.
