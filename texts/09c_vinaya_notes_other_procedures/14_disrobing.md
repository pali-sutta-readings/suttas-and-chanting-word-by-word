# Disrobing

> After the bhikkhus who are to witness the disrobing have assembled, and he has confessed his offences and asked for forgiveness, the bhikkhu who will disrobe, wearing all his three robes, with his saṅghāti on his left shoulder, bows three times and chants:

**Namo tassa bhagavato arahato sammā-sambuddhassa (×3)**

**Literal:** Homage to that Blessed One, Arahant, Perfectly Self-Awakened One  
**Faithful:** Homage to the Blessed One, the Arahant, the Perfectly Self-Awakened One.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`namo`** | `(ind)` homage, reverence (to); **[√nam + as + o]** |
| **`tassa`** | `(dem pron masc dat sg)` to that, to him; **[ta]** |
| **`bhagavato`** | `(masc dat sg)` to the Blessed One; **[√bhaj > bhag + a + vant]** |
| **`arahato`** | `(masc dat sg)` to the Arahant, to the Worthy One; **[√arah + ant]** |
| **`sammā-sambuddhassa`** | `(masc dat sg comp)` to the Perfectly Self-Awakened One; **[sammā + sambuddha]** |
| ↳ **`sammā`** | `(ind comp)` perfectly, rightly; **[sammā]** |
| ↳ **`sambuddhassa`** | `(masc dat sg)` to the self-awakened one; **[saṁ + √budh + ta]** |

*(Bow three times)*

> He recites in Pāli and in his own language:

**Sikkhaṁ paccakkhāmi. Gihī'ti maṁ dhāretha.**

**Literal:** [The] training I renounce. [A] householder [thus] me [you] should regard.  
**Faithful:** I give up the training. May you regard me as a layman.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`sikkhaṁ`** | `(fem acc sg)` training; **[√sikkh + ā]** |
| **`paccakkhāmi`** | `(1st sg pres act indic)` I renounce, I give up, I disavow; **[pati > paty > pacc + ā + √khā + mi]** |
| **`gihī'ti`** | `(sandhi)` [*gihī* (masc nom sg) householder, layman + *iti* (ind), marking how one is regarded] as a layman; **[√gah > geh > gih + a + ī; iti]** |
| **`maṁ`** | `(1st sg acc)` me; **[ahaṁ]** |
| **`dhāretha`** | `(2nd pl pres act imperat)` regard, consider, remember (me as); **[√dhar + e > dhāre + tha]** |
