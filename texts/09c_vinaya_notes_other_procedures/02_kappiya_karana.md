# Kappiya-karaṇa

*Making Fruit Allowable*

> For fruit with seeds or vegetables that can grow again, the bhikkhu says:

**Kappiyaṁ karohi.**

**Literal:** Allowable make.  
**Faithful:** Make it allowable.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`kappiyaṁ`** | `(ptp nt acc sg)` [predicate accusative] allowable, suitable; **[√kapp + iya]** |
| **`karohi`** | `(2nd sg pres act imperat)` make!, do!; **[√kar > karo + hi]** |

> The lay person, while marking (cutting, tearing or burning) the fruit, responds:

**Kappiyaṁ bhante.**

**Literal:** Allowable, venerable sir.  
**Faithful:** It is allowable, ven. sir.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`kappiyaṁ`** | `(ptp nt nom sg)` [*it is* understood] allowable, suitable; **[√kapp + iya]** |
| **`bhante`** | `(masc voc sg)` venerable sir; **[√bhū > bhava + nta + e]** |

*Sp.IV.767–768*
