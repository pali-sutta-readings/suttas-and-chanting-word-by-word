# Taking Leave after Uposatha

> Having undertaken the Eight Precepts and stayed overnight at the monastery, lay followers take their leave from the senior monk the next morning. Laypeople:

**Handa dāni mayaṁ bhante āpucchāma**

**Literal:** Well now, we, venerable sir, take leave;  
**Faithful:** Well now, venerable sir, we take our leave;  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`handa`** | `(interj)` come now, well then; **[handa]** |
| **`dāni`** | `(ind)` now; **[ima + dāni]** |
| **`mayaṁ`** | `(1st pl nom)` we; **[ahaṁ]** |
| **`bhante`** | `(masc voc sg)` venerable sir; **[√bhū > bhava + nta + e]** |
| **`āpucchāma`** | `(1st pl pres act indic)` we take leave, we ask permission; **[ā + √pucch + a + ma]** |

**bahukiccā mayaṁ bahukaraṇīyā**

**Literal:** having many duties [are] we, having much to do.  
**Faithful:** we have many duties and much to do.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`bahukiccā`** | `(adj masc nom pl comp)` with many duties, busy; **[bahu + kicca]** |
| ↳ **`bahu`** | `(adj comp)` many, much; **[√bah + u]** |
| ↳ **`kiccā`** | `(adj masc nom pl)` duties, things to be done; **[√kar > kir + tya]** |
| **`mayaṁ`** | `(1st pl nom)` we; **[ahaṁ]** |
| **`bahukaraṇīyā`** | `(adj masc nom pl comp)` with much to do, busy; **[bahu + karaṇīya]** |
| ↳ **`bahu`** | `(adj comp)` many, much; **[√bah + u]** |
| ↳ **`karaṇīyā`** | `(adj masc nom pl)` things to be done, duties; **[√kar + aṇīya]** |

> Senior monk:

**Yassa dāni tumhe kālaṁ maññatha.**

**Literal:** Of which now you [the] time think.  
**Faithful:** Please do what is appropriate at this time.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`yassa`** | `(rel pron nt dat/gen sg)` [*yassa … kālaṁ*: the time for which] for which, of which; **[ya]** |
| **`dāni`** | `(ind)` now; **[ima + dāni]** |
| **`tumhe`** | `(2nd pl nom)` you; **[tumha]** |
| **`kālaṁ`** | `(masc acc sg)` time, the right time; **[√kal > kāl + a]** |
| **`maññatha`** | `(2nd pl pres act indic)` [idiom: you think it is time (for)] you think, you consider; **[√man + ya > mañña + tha]** |
