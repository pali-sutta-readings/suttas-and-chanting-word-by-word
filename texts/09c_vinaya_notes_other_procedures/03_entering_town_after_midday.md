# Entering Town after Midday

> Leave can be taken in one's own language, or in Pāli:

**Vikāle gāmappavesanaṁ āpucchāmi.**

**Literal:** At [the] wrong time entering [the] village I ask leave [for].  
**Faithful:** I take leave to go to the town at the wrong time.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`vikāle`** | `(masc loc sg)` at the wrong time (from midday until dawn), in the afternoon; **[vi + √kal > kāl + a]** |
| **`gāmappavesanaṁ`** | `(nt acc sg comp)` entering the village or town; **[gāma + pavesana]** |
| ↳ **`gāma`** | `(masc comp)` village, town; **[gāma, no root in DPD]** |
| ↳ **`ppavesanaṁ`** | `(nt acc sg)` [*pavesana* with *p* doubled after the first member] entering, going into; **[pa + √vis > vese + ana]** |
| **`āpucchāmi`** | `(1st sg pres act indic)` I ask permission, I take leave; **[ā + √pucch + a + mi]** |
