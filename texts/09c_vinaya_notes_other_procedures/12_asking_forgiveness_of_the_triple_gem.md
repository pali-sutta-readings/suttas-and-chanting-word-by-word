# Asking Forgiveness of the Triple Gem

**Ahaṁ buddhañ ca dhammañ ca saṅghañ ca saraṇaṁ gato**

**Literal:** I [to the] Buddha and [the] Dhamma and [the] Saṅgha and [as] refuge gone,  
**Faithful:** Having gone to the Buddha, the Dhamma and the Saṅgha for refuge, I declared myself a lay follower in the presence of the Community of bhikkhus.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`ahaṁ`** | `(1st sg nom)` I; **[ahaṁ]** |
| **`buddhañ`** | `(masc acc sg)` [*buddhaṁ* before *c-*] the Buddha; **[√budh + ta]** |
| **`ca`** | `(ind)` and; **[ca]** |
| **`dhammañ`** | `(masc acc sg)` [*dhammaṁ* before *c-*] the Dhamma; **[√dhar + ma]** |
| **`ca`** | `(ind)` and; **[ca]** |
| **`saṅghañ`** | `(masc acc sg)` [*saṅghaṁ* before *c-*] the Saṅgha; **[saṁ + √ghaṭ + a]** |
| **`ca`** | `(ind)` and; **[ca]** |
| **`saraṇaṁ`** | `(nt acc sg)` [predicate accusative] [as] refuge; **[√sar + aṇa]** |
| **`gato`** | `(pp masc nom sg)` gone; **[√gam + ta]** |

**upāsakattaṁ desesiṁ bhikkhu-saṅghassa sammukhā.**

**Literal:** lay-followership I declared of [the] bhikkhu-Community in [the] presence.  
**Faithful:** (continued)  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`upāsakattaṁ`** | `(nt acc sg)` the state of a lay follower, being a lay disciple; **[upa + √ās + aka + tta]** |
| **`desesiṁ`** | `(1st sg aor caus act indic)` I declared, I made known; **[√dis > dese + siṁ]** |
| **`bhikkhu-saṅghassa`** | `(masc gen sg comp)` of the Community of bhikkhus; **[bhikkhu + saṅgha]** |
| ↳ **`bhikkhu`** | `(masc comp)` bhikkhu, monk; **[√bhikkh + u]** |
| ↳ **`saṅghassa`** | `(masc gen sg)` of the community; **[saṁ + √ghaṭ + a]** |
| **`sammukhā`** | `(ind)` [with the genitive] in the presence (of), face to face (with); **[saṁ + mukha + ā]** |

**Variant:** women chant: *gato* → *gatā*; *upāsakattaṁ* → *upāsikattaṁ*  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`gatā`** | `(pp fem nom sg)` gone; **[√gam + ta]** |
| **`upāsikattaṁ`** | `(nt acc sg)` [not in DPD; from *upāsikā*, as *upāsakatta* from *upāsaka*] the state of a female lay follower; **[upa + √ās + ikā + tta]** |

**etaṁ saraṇam āgamma sabba-dukkhā pamuccaye.**

**Literal:** this refuge having come to, from all suffering one may be released.  
**Faithful:** having come to this refuge, one may be released from all suffering.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`etaṁ`** | `(dem pron nt acc sg)` this; **[eta]** |
| **`saraṇam`** | `(nt acc sg)` [*saraṇaṁ* before a vowel] refuge; **[√sar + aṇa]** |
| **`āgamma`** | `(abs)` having come to, relying on; **[ā + √gam + ya]** |
| **`sabba-dukkhā`** | `(nt abl sg comp)` from all suffering; **[sabba + dukkha]** |
| ↳ **`sabba`** | `(pron comp)` all; **[sabba]** |
| ↳ **`dukkhā`** | `(nt abl sg)` from suffering; **[√dukkh + a]** |
| **`pamuccaye`** | `(3rd sg pass opt)` [not in DPD; optative of *pamuccati*, passive of *pamuñcati*, with the *-aye* ending as in *visodhaye*] may be released, would be freed; **[pa + √muc + ya > mucca + e]** |

**Yathā-balaṁ careyyāhaṁ sammā-sambuddha-sāsanaṁ**

**Literal:** According to [my] strength may I practise [the] Perfectly-Self-Awakened-One's teaching;  
**Faithful:** According to my strength, may I practise the teaching of the Perfectly Self-Awakened One;  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`yathā-balaṁ`** | `(ind)` according to one's strength, according to one's means; **[yathā + bala + aṁ]** |
| **`careyyāhaṁ`** | `(sandhi)` [*careyyaṁ* (1st sg act opt) may I practise + *ahaṁ* (1st sg nom) I] may I practise; **[√car + eyyaṁ; ahaṁ]** |
| **`sammā-sambuddha-sāsanaṁ`** | `(nt acc sg comp)` the teaching of the Perfectly Self-Awakened One; **[sammā + sambuddha + sāsana]** |
| ↳ **`sammā`** | `(ind comp)` perfectly, rightly; **[sammā]** |
| ↳ **`sambuddha`** | `(masc comp)` self-awakened one; **[saṁ + √budh + ta]** |
| ↳ **`sāsanaṁ`** | `(nt acc sg)` teaching, dispensation; **[√sās + ana]** |

**dukkha-nissaraṇass' eva bhāgī assaṁ anāgate.**

**Literal:** of [the] escape from suffering indeed [a] sharer may I be in [the] future.  
**Faithful:** may I in future be a sharer in the escape from suffering.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`dukkha-nissaraṇass'`** | `(nt gen sg comp)` [*dukkha-nissaraṇassa* before a vowel] of the escape from suffering; **[dukkha + nissaraṇa]** |
| ↳ **`dukkha`** | `(nt comp)` suffering; **[√dukkh + a]** |
| ↳ **`nissaraṇass'`** | `(nt gen sg)` [*nissaraṇassa* before a vowel] of the escape, of the way out; **[nī + √sar + aṇa]** |
| **`eva`** | `(ind)` indeed, just; **[eva]** |
| **`bhāgī`** | `(adj masc nom sg)` sharer (in), partaking (of); **[√bhaj > bhag > bhāg + ī]** |
| **`assaṁ`** | `(1st sg act opt)` may I be; **[√as + aṁ]** |
| **`anāgate`** | `(pp nt loc sg)` [used as a noun] in the future; **[na > an + ā + √gam + ta]** |

**Variant:** women chant: *bhāgī assaṁ* → *bhāginissaṁ*  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`bhāginissaṁ`** | `(sandhi)` [*bhāginī* (adj fem nom sg) sharer + *assaṁ* (1st sg act opt) may I be] may I be a sharer; **[√bhaj > bhag > bhāg + inī; √as + aṁ]** |

**Buddho paṭiggaṇhātu accayantaṁ**

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
