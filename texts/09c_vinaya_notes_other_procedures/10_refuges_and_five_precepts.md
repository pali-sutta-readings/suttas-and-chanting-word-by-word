# Requesting the Three Refuges & the Five Precepts

> In the Thai tradition, after bowing three times, with hands joined in añjali, recite the appropriate request. For a group from a monk:

**Mayaṁ bhante tisaraṇena saha pañca sīlāni yācāma**

**Literal:** We, venerable sir, [the] Three Refuges together with, [the] five precepts request.  
**Faithful:** We, Venerable Sir, request the Three Refuges and the Five Precepts.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`mayaṁ`** | `(1st pl nom)` we; **[ahaṁ]** |
| **`bhante`** | `(masc voc sg)` venerable sir; **[√bhū > bhava + nta + e]** |
| **`tisaraṇena`** | `(nt instr sg comp)` [instrumental with *saha*] the Three Refuges, the Triple Gem; **[ti + saraṇa]** |
| ↳ **`ti`** | `(card comp)` three; **[ti]** |
| ↳ **`saraṇena`** | `(nt instr sg)` refuge; **[√sar + aṇa]** |
| **`saha`** | `(ind)` together with, with; **[saha]** |
| **`pañca`** | `(card nt acc pl)` five; **[pañca]** |
| **`sīlāni`** | `(nt acc pl)` precepts, virtues, ethical conduct; **[√sīl + a]** |
| **`yācāma`** | `(1st pl pres act indic)` we request, we ask for; **[√yāc + a + ma]** |
