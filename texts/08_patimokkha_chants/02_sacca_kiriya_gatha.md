# Sacca-kiriyā-gāthā

*Verses of the Act of Truth*

**Handa mayaṁ sacca-kiriyā-gāthāyo bhaṇāmase**

**Literal:** Come now, we [the] truth-act-verses let us recite  
**Faithful:** Now let us recite the verses of the act of truth.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`handa`** | `(interj)` come now, well then; **[handa]** |
| **`mayaṁ`** | `(1st pl nom)` we; **[ahaṁ]** |
| **`sacca-kiriyā-gāthāyo`** | `(fem acc pl comp)` verses of the act of truth; **[sacca + kiriyā + gāthā]** |
| ↳ **`sacca`** | `(nt comp)` truth; **[sat + ya (√as)]** |
| ↳ **`kiriyā`** | `(fem comp)` act, making, doing; **[√kar > kir + iyā]** |
| ↳ **`gāthāyo`** | `(fem acc pl)` verses; **[√gā + thā]** |
| **`bhaṇāmase`** | `(1st pl pres mid imperat)` let us recite, let us say; **[√bhaṇ + a + mase]** |

**Natthi me saraṇaṁ aññaṁ buddho me saraṇaṁ varaṁ**

**Literal:** There is not for me refuge other; [the] Buddha [is] my refuge excellent  
**Faithful:** I have no other refuge; the Buddha is my excellent refuge.

| **`aññaṁ`** | `(pron nt nom sg)` other, another; **[añña]** |

**Etena sacca-vajjena sotthi me hotu sabbadā**

**Literal:** By this truth-speaking safety for me may be always.  
**Faithful:** By the speaking of this truth, may I be safe at all times.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`etena`** | `(dem pron nt instr sg)` by this; **[eta]** |
| **`sacca-vajjena`** | `(nt instr sg comp)` by the speaking of truth, by a declaration of truth; **[sacca + vajja]** |
| ↳ **`sacca`** | `(nt comp)` truth; **[sat + ya (√as)]** |
| ↳ **`vajjena`** | `(ptp nt instr sg)` speaking, what is said; **[√vad + ya]** |
| **`sotthi`** | `(fem nom sg)` safety, well-being; **[su > so + √as + ti]** |
| **`me`** | `(1st sg dat)` for me; **[ahaṁ]** |
| **`hotu`** | `(3rd sg pres act imperat)` may it be, may there be; **[√hū > ho + tu]** |
| **`sabbadā`** | `(ind)` always, at all times; **[sabba + dā]** |
