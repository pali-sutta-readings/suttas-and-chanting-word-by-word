# Nissaggiya Pācittiya 22

*New Bowl*

> To be forfeited to the Saṅgha only:

**Ayaṁ me bhante patto ūnapañca-bandhanena pattena cetāpito nissaggiyo. Imāhaṁ saṅghassa nissajjāmi.**

**Literal:** This by me, venerable sirs, bowl, for [a] less-than-five-mends bowl exchanged, [is] to be forfeited. This I to [the] Community forfeit.  
**Faithful:** This bowl, ven. sirs, which has been exchanged for a bowl that has less than five mends, is to be forfeited by me: I forfeit it to the Sangha.
