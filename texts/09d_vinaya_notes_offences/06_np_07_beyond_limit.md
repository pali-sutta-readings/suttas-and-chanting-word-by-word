# Nissaggiya Pācittiya 7

*Beyond Limit*

**Idaṁ me bhante cīvaraṁ aññātakaṁ gahapatikaṁ upasaṁkamitvā tat'uttariṁ viññāpitaṁ nissaggiyaṁ. Imāhaṁ āyasmato nissajjāmi.**

**Literal:** This by me, venerable sir, robe, [an] unrelated householder having approached, beyond that asked for, [is] to be forfeited. This I to [the] venerable [one] forfeit.  
**Faithful:** This robe, ven. sir, which has been asked for beyond the limitation from an unrelated householder, is to be forfeited by me: I forfeit it to you.

> For more than one piece of cloth:

**Imāni me bhante cīvarāni aññātakaṁ gahapatikaṁ upasaṁkamitvā tat'uttariṁ viññāpitāni nissaggiyāni. Imānāhaṁ āyasmato nissajjāmi.**

**Literal:** These by me, venerable sir, robes, [an] unrelated householder having approached, beyond that asked for, [are] to be forfeited. These I to [the] venerable [one] forfeit.

> Returning the robe:

**Imaṁ cīvaraṁ āyasmato dammi.**

**Literal:** This robe to [the] venerable [one] [I] give.  
**Faithful:** I give this robe to you.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
