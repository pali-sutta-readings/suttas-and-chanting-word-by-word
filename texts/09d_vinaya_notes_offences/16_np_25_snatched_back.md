# Nissaggiya Pācittiya 25

*Snatched Back*

**Idaṁ me bhante cīvaraṁ bhikkhussa sāmaṁ datvā acchinnaṁ nissaggiyaṁ. Imāhaṁ āyasmato nissajjāmi.**

**Literal:** This by me, venerable sir, robe, to [a] bhikkhu oneself having given, snatched back, [is] to be forfeited. This I to [the] venerable [one] forfeit.  
**Faithful:** This robe, ven. sir, which has been snatched back after having given it myself to a bhikkhu, is to be forfeited by me: I forfeit it to you.
