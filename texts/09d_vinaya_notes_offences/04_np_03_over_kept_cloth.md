# Nissaggiya Pācittiya 3

*Over-kept Cloth*

**Idaṁ me bhante akāla-cīvaraṁ māsātikkantaṁ nissaggiyaṁ. Imāhaṁ āyasmato nissajjāmi.**

**Literal:** This by me, venerable sir, out-of-season robe, beyond a month, [is] to be forfeited. This I to [the] venerable [one] forfeit.  
**Faithful:** This, ven. sir, 'out of season' robe, which has passed beyond the month (limit), is to be forfeited by me: I forfeit it to you.

| **`idaṁ`** | `(dem pron nt nom sg)` this; **[ima]** |

> Returning the robe:

**Imaṁ cīvaraṁ āyasmato dammi.**

**Literal:** This robe to [the] venerable [one] [I] give.  
**Faithful:** I give this robe to you.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`imaṁ`** | `(dem pron nt acc sg)` this; **[ima]** |
| **`cīvaraṁ`** | `(nt acc sg)` robe, robe-cloth; **[cīvara, no root in DPD]** |
| **`āyasmato`** | `(masc dat sg)` to the venerable one, to you; **[ā + √i > ay + as + mant]** |
| **`dammi`** | `(1st sg pres act indic)` I give; **[√dā + āmi > dammi]** |

**Variant:** returning more than one piece of cloth: *imaṁ* → *imāni*; *cīvaraṁ* → *cīvarāni*
