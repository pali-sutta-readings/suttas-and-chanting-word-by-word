# Nissaggiya Pācittiya 28

*Urgent*

**Idaṁ me bhante acceka-cīvaraṁ cīvara-kālasamayaṁ atikkāmitaṁ nissaggiyaṁ. Imāhaṁ āyasmato nissajjāmi.**

**Literal:** This by me, venerable sir, urgent robe, [the] robe-season kept beyond, [is] to be forfeited. This I to [the] venerable [one] forfeit.  
**Faithful:** This robe-offered-in-urgency, ven. sir, has passed beyond the robe-season, is to be forfeited by me: I forfeit it to you.
