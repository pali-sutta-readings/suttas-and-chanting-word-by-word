# Nissaggiya Pācittiya 9

*Instructing*

> For a robe (robe-cloth) received after making instructions to two or more householders:

**Idaṁ me bhante cīvaraṁ pubbe appavārito aññātake gahapatike upasaṅkamitvā cīvare vikappaṁ āpannaṁ nissaggiyaṁ. Imāhaṁ āyasmato nissajjāmi.**

**Literal:** This by me, venerable sir, robe, beforehand not invited, unrelated householders having approached, about [the] robe [a] stipulation entered into, [is] to be forfeited. This I to [the] venerable [one] forfeit.  
**Faithful:** This robe, ven. sir, which has been instructed about after having approached unrelated householders without prior invitation is to be forfeited by me: I forfeit it to you.

> Returning the robe:

**Imaṁ cīvaraṁ āyasmato dammi.**

**Literal:** This robe to [the] venerable [one] [I] give.  
**Faithful:** I give this robe to you.
