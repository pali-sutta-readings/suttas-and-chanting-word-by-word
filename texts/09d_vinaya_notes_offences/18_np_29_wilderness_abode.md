# Nissaggiya Pācittiya 29

*Wilderness Abode*

**Idaṁ me bhante cīvaraṁ atireka-chā-rattaṁ vippavutthaṁ aññatra bhikkhu-sammatiyā nissaggiyaṁ. Imāhaṁ āyasmato nissajjāmi.**

**Literal:** This by me, venerable sir, robe, [for] more than six nights separated, except [with the] bhikkhus' consent, [is] to be forfeited. This I to [the] venerable [one] forfeit.  
**Faithful:** This robe, ven. sir, which has stayed separate (from me) for more than six nights without the consent of the bhikkhus, is to be forfeited by me: I forfeit it to you.
