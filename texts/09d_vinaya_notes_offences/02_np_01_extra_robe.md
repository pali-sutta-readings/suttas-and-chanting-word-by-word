# Nissaggiya Pācittiya 1

*Extra Robe*

> On the eleventh dawn of keeping one extra robe, within forearm's length, forfeiting to a more senior bhikkhu:

**Idaṁ me bhante cīvaraṁ dasāhātikkantaṁ nissaggiyaṁ. Imāhaṁ āyasmato nissajjāmi.**

**Literal:** This by me, venerable sir, robe, beyond ten days, [is] to be forfeited. This I to [the] venerable [one] forfeit.  
**Faithful:** This extra robe, ven. sir, which has passed beyond the ten day (limit), is to be forfeited by me: I forfeit it to you.

| **`idaṁ`** | `(dem pron nt nom sg)` this; **[ima]** |
| **`me`** | `(1st sg instr)` [enclitic; agent of *nissaggiyaṁ*] by me; **[ahaṁ]** |

| **`cīvaraṁ`** | `(nt nom sg)` robe, robe-cloth; **[cīvara, no root in DPD]** |

**Imāni me bhante cīvarāni dasāhātikkantāni nissaggiyāni. Imānāhaṁ āyasmato nissajjāmi.**

> Returning the robe:

**Imaṁ cīvaraṁ āyasmato dammi.**

**Literal:** This robe to [the] venerable [one] [I] give.  
**Faithful:** I give this robe to you.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
