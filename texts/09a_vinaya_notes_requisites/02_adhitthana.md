# Adhiṭṭhāna

*Determining*

> Articles are determined either by touching the article and mentally reciting the relevant Pāli passage, or by uttering the Pāli passage without touching the article:

**Imaṁ saṅghāṭiṁ adhiṭṭhāmi.**

**Literal:** This outer-robe [I] determine.  
**Faithful:** I determine this outer robe.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`imaṁ`** | `(dem pron fem acc sg)` this; **[ima]** |
| **`saṅghāṭiṁ`** | `(fem acc sg)` outer robe, double robe; **[saṁ + √ghaṭ > ghāṭ + i]** |
| **`adhiṭṭhāmi`** | `(1st sg pres act indic)` I determine, I formally resolve on (for use); **[adhi + √ṭhā + a + mi]** |

**Variant:** upper robe: *imaṁ saṅghāṭiṁ* → *imaṁ uttarā-saṅgaṁ*  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`imaṁ`** | `(dem pron masc acc sg)` [agrees with *uttarā-saṅgaṁ*] this; **[ima]** |
| **`uttarā-saṅgaṁ`** | `(masc acc sg comp)` [BM's hyphen; DPD divides *uttara + āsaṅga*] upper robe; **[uttara + āsaṅga]** |
| ↳ **`uttara`** | `(adj comp)` upper, higher; **[ud + tara]** |
| ↳ **`āsaṅgaṁ`** | `(masc acc sg)` robe, cloak; **[ā + √sañj > saṅg + a]** |

> When several articles of one of the last three kinds (sleeping cloths, handkerchiefs, small requisites) are determined together, e.g.:

**Imāni paccattharaṇāni adhiṭṭhāmi.**

**Literal:** These sleeping-cloths [I] determine.  
**Faithful:** I determine these sleeping cloths.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
