# Vikappana

*Sharing Ownership: Generally Addressing the Recipient*

> In the presence of the receiving bhikkhu, and with the article within forearm's length:

**Imaṁ cīvaraṁ tuyhaṁ vikappemi.**

**Literal:** This robe to-you [I] share.  
**Faithful:** I share this robe with you.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`imaṁ`** | `(dem pron nt acc sg)` this; **[ima]** |
| **`cīvaraṁ`** | `(nt acc sg)` robe, robe-cloth; **[cīvara, no root in DPD]** |
| **`tuyhaṁ`** | `(2nd sg dat)` to you, with you; **[tvaṁ]** |
| **`vikappemi`** | `(1st sg pres caus act indic)` I share (ownership), I assign (ownership) to; **[vi + √kapp + e + mi]** |

**Imāni cīvarāni tuyhaṁ vikappemi.**

**Literal:** These robes to-you [I] share.  
**Faithful:** I share these robes with you.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`imāni`** | `(dem pron nt acc pl)` these; **[ima]** |
| **`cīvarāni`** | `(nt acc pl)` robes, robe-cloths; **[cīvara, no root in DPD]** |
| **`tuyhaṁ`** | `(2nd sg dat)` to you, with you; **[tvaṁ]** |
| **`vikappemi`** | `(1st sg pres caus act indic)` I share (ownership), I assign (ownership) to; **[vi + √kapp + e + mi]** |

**Imaṁ pattaṁ tuyhaṁ vikappemi.**

**Literal:** This bowl to-you [I] share.  
**Faithful:** I share this bowl with you.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`imaṁ`** | `(dem pron masc acc sg)` this; **[ima]** |
| **`pattaṁ`** | `(masc acc sg)` alms bowl, bowl; **[√pā + ta]** |
| **`tuyhaṁ`** | `(2nd sg dat)` to you, with you; **[tvaṁ]** |
| **`vikappemi`** | `(1st sg pres caus act indic)` I share (ownership), I assign (ownership) to; **[vi + √kapp + e + mi]** |

**Ime patte tuyhaṁ vikappemi.**

**Literal:** These bowls to-you [I] share.  
**Faithful:** I share these bowls with you.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`ime`** | `(dem pron masc acc pl)` these; **[ima]** |
| **`patte`** | `(masc acc pl)` alms bowls, bowls; **[√pā + ta]** |
| **`tuyhaṁ`** | `(2nd sg dat)` to you, with you; **[tvaṁ]** |
| **`vikappemi`** | `(1st sg pres caus act indic)` I share (ownership), I assign (ownership) to; **[vi + √kapp + e + mi]** |

**Variant:** receiving bhikkhu is the senior: *tuyhaṁ* → *āyasmato*  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`āyasmato`** | `(masc dat sg)` to the venerable one, to you, venerable sir; **[ā + √i > ay + as + mant]** |

**Variant:** shared with more than one bhikkhu: *tuyhaṁ* → *tumhākaṁ*  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`tumhākaṁ`** | `(2nd pl dat)` to you (all), with you (all); **[tumha + ākaṁ]** |

**Variant:** article beyond forearm's length: *imaṁ* → *etaṁ*; *imāni* → *etāni*; *ime* → *ete*  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`etaṁ`** | `(dem pron nt acc sg)` / `(dem pron masc acc sg)` [nt with *cīvaraṁ*, masc with *pattaṁ*] that; **[eta]** |
| **`etāni`** | `(dem pron nt acc pl)` those; **[eta]** |
| **`ete`** | `(dem pron masc acc pl)` those; **[eta]** |
