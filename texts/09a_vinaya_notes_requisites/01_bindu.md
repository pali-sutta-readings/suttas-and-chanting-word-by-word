# Bindu

*Marking*

> Before use, a new robe must be marked with three dots, blue, green, black or brown in colour, saying, either out loud or mentally:

**Imaṁ bindukappaṁ karomi. (×3)**

**Literal:** This dot-mark [I] make.  
**Faithful:** I make this properly marked.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`imaṁ`** | `(dem pron masc acc sg)` this; **[ima]** |
| **`bindukappaṁ`** | `(masc acc sg comp)` dot-mark, marking with a dot (that makes a robe allowable); **[bindu + kappa]** |
| ↳ **`bindu`** | `(nt comp)` dot, spot; **[bindu, no root in DPD]** |
| ↳ **`kappaṁ`** | `(masc acc sg)` (vinaya) distinguishing mark on a robe; **[√kapp + a]** |
| **`karomi`** | `(1st sg pres act indic)` I make, I do; **[√kar > karo + mi]** |
