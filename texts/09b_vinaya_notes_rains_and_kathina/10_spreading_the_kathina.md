# Spreading the Kaṭhina

> After the Kaṭhina robe has been sewn and dyed, and the old robe relinquished, the new robe is marked and determined. Then the recipient chants:

**Namo tassa bhagavato arahato sammāsambuddhassa (×3)**

**Literal:** Homage to that Blessed One, Arahant, Perfectly Self-Awakened One  
**Faithful:** Homage to the Blessed One, the Arahant, the Perfectly Self-Awakened One.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`namo`** | `(ind)` homage, reverence (to); **[√nam + as + o]** |
| **`tassa`** | `(dem pron masc dat sg)` to that, to him; **[ta]** |
| **`bhagavato`** | `(masc dat sg)` to the Blessed One; **[√bhaj > bhag + a + vant]** |
| **`arahato`** | `(masc dat sg)` to the Arahant, to the Worthy One; **[√arah + ant]** |
| **`sammāsambuddhassa`** | `(masc dat sg comp)` to the Perfectly Self-Awakened One; **[sammā + sambuddha]** |
| ↳ **`sammā`** | `(ind comp)` perfectly, rightly; **[sammā]** |
| ↳ **`sambuddhassa`** | `(masc dat sg)` to the self-awakened one; **[saṁ + √budh + ta]** |

> Then *one* of the following:

**Imāya saṅghāṭiyā kaṭhinaṁ attharāmi.**

**Literal:** By this outer robe [the] Kaṭhina I spread.  
**Faithful:** By means of this outer robe I spread the Kaṭhina.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`imāya`** | `(dem pron fem instr sg)` [agrees with *saṅghāṭiyā*] by this, with this; **[ima]** |
| **`saṅghāṭiyā`** | `(fem instr sg)` by means of the outer robe, with the double robe; **[saṁ + √ghaṭ > ghāṭ + i]** |
| **`kaṭhinaṁ`** | `(nt acc sg)` the Kaṭhina (the robe-making privileges after the Rains); **[kaṭhina, no root in DPD]** |
| **`attharāmi`** | `(1st sg pres act indic)` I spread, I lay out; **[ā + √thar + a + mi]** |

**Variant:** by means of the upper robe: *Imāya saṅghāṭiyā* → *Iminā uttarāsaṅgena*  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`iminā`** | `(dem pron masc instr sg)` [agrees with *uttarāsaṅgena*] by this, with this; **[ima]** |
| **`uttarāsaṅgena`** | `(masc instr sg comp)` by means of the upper robe; **[uttara + āsaṅga]** |
| ↳ **`uttar'`** | `(adj comp)` upper, outer; **[ud + tara]** |
| ↳ **`āsaṅgena`** | `(masc instr sg)` robe, cloak; **[ā + √sañj > saṅg + a]** |

**Variant:** by means of the lower robe: *Imāya saṅghāṭiyā* → *Iminā antaravāsakena*  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`iminā`** | `(dem pron masc instr sg)` [agrees with *antaravāsakena*] by this, with this; **[ima]** |
| **`antaravāsakena`** | `(masc instr sg comp)` by means of the lower robe, with the inner robe; **[antara + vāsaka]** |
| ↳ **`antara`** | `(adj comp)` inner, inside; **[anta + ra]** |
| ↳ **`vāsakena`** | `(masc instr sg)` garment, clothing; **[√vas > vās + a + ka]** |
