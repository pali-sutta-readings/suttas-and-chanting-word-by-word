# Kaṭhina Saṅghakamma

*Giving the Kaṭhina-cloth*

> When the sewing has been completed, the bhikkhus meet inside the *sīmā*, bow to the shrine, and chant the Dedication of Offerings and the Preliminary Homage.

> The chanting bhikkhu announces the motion and decision; here Amaro Bhikkhu is the receiving senior bhikkhu:

**Suṇātu me bhante saṅgho.**

**Literal:** May listen [to] me, venerable sirs, [the] Community.  
**Faithful:** Venerable sirs, may the Community listen to me.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`suṇātu`** | `(3rd sg pres act imperat)` may (it) listen, let (it) hear; **[√su + ṇā + tu]** |
| **`me`** | `(1st sg gen)` [genitive with a verb of hearing] to me, me; **[ahaṁ]** |
| **`bhante`** | `(masc voc pl)` venerable sirs; **[√bhū > bhava + nta + e]** |
| **`saṅgho`** | `(masc nom sg)` Community, Saṅgha; **[saṁ + √ghaṭ + a]** |

**Idaṁ saṅghassa kaṭhina-dussaṁ uppannaṁ.**

**Faithful:** This Kaṭhina-cloth has arisen for the Community.

**Yass'āyasmato khamati, imassa kaṭhina-dussassa āyasmato Amarassa dānaṁ, kaṭhinaṁ attharituṁ, so tuṇh'assa.**
