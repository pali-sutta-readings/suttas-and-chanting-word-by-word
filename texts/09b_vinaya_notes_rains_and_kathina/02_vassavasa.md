# Vassāvāsa

*Entering the Rains (Thai tradition)*

> After the boundaries are specified, all resident bhikkhus together:

**Imasmiṁ āvāse imaṁ te-māsaṁ vassaṁ upema. (×3)**

**Literal:** In this residence this three-month Rains we enter.  
**Faithful:** We enter the Rains in this monastery for three months.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`imasmiṁ`** | `(dem pron masc loc sg)` [agrees with *āvāse*] in this; **[ima]** |
| **`āvāse`** | `(masc loc sg)` in the residence, in the dwelling place, in the monastery; **[ā + √vas > vās + a]** |
| **`imaṁ`** | `(dem pron masc acc sg)` [agrees with *vassaṁ*] this; **[ima]** |
| **`te-māsaṁ`** | `(nt acc sg comp)` [accusative of duration] for three months, the period of three months; **[ti > te + māsa + a]** |
| ↳ **`te`** | `(card comp)` three; **[ti]** |
| ↳ **`māsaṁ`** | `(nt acc sg)` month; **[√mā + sa]** |
| **`vassaṁ`** | `(masc acc sg)` the Rains, the rainy season; **[√vass + a]** |
| **`upema`** | `(1st pl pres act indic)` we enter (upon), we undertake; lit. we approach; **[upa + √i + a > e + ma]** |

**Variant:** one bhikkhu at a time: *upema* → *upemi*  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`upemi`** | `(1st sg pres act indic)` I enter (upon), I undertake; **[upa + √i + a > e + mi]** |
