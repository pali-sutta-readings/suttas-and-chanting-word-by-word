# Ānāpānassati-sutta

*The Discourse on Mindfulness of Breathing*

**Literal:** Come now, we the mindfulness-of-breathing-discourse-passage let us recite.  
**Faithful:** Come now, let us recite the passage of the Discourse on Mindfulness of Breathing.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
