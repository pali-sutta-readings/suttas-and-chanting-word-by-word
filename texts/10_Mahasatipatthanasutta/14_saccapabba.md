# Saccapabba

*The Noble Truths*

### 4.5 Dhammānupassanāsaccapabba

*Contemplation of dhammas: the section on the noble truths*

**Puna caparaṁ, bhikkhave, bhikkhu dhammesu dhammānupassī viharati catūsu ariyasaccesu.**

**Literal:** Again and-further, bhikkhus, a-bhikkhu in-dhammas dhamma-contemplating dwells in-[the]-four noble-truths.  
**Faithful:** Again, bhikkhus, a bhikkhu dwells contemplating dhammas as dhammas with regard to the four noble truths.

| :--- | :--- |
| **`puna`** | `(ind)` again, further; **[puna]** |
| **`caparaṁ`** | `(sandhi)` [*ca* (ind) + *paraṁ* (adv)] and further, and what is more; **[ca + paraṁ]** |

| **`dhammānupassī`** | `(adj masc nom sg comp)` contemplating dhammas; **[dhamma + anupassī]** |
| ↳ **`dhamm'`** | `(masc comp)` [*dhamma* before a vowel] dhamma, mental quality; **[√dhar + ma]** |

**Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu idaṁ dukkhan'ti yathābhūtaṁ pajānāti,**

**Literal:** Here, bhikkhus, a-bhikkhu, "this [is] suffering" thus as-it-really-is understands;  
**Faithful:** Here, bhikkhus, a bhikkhu understands as it really is: "This is suffering";
