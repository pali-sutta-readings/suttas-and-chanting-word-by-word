# Āyatanapabba

*The Sense Bases*

### 4.3 Dhammānupassanāāyatanapabba

*Contemplation of dhammas: the section on the sense bases*

**Puna caparaṁ, bhikkhave, bhikkhu dhammesu dhammānupassī viharati chasu ajjhattikabāhiresu āyatanesu.**

**Literal:** Again and-further, bhikkhus, a-bhikkhu in-dhammas dhamma-contemplating dwells in-[the]-six internal-and-external sense-bases.  
**Faithful:** Again, bhikkhus, a bhikkhu dwells contemplating dhammas as dhammas with regard to the six internal and external sense bases.

**Iti ajjhattaṁ vā dhammesu dhammānupassī viharati,**

**Literal:** Thus internally or in-dhammas dhamma-contemplating he-dwells,  
**Faithful:** In this way he dwells contemplating dhammas as dhammas internally,

**bahiddhā vā dhammesu dhammānupassī viharati,**

**Literal:** externally or in-dhammas dhamma-contemplating he-dwells,  
**Faithful:** or he dwells contemplating dhammas as dhammas externally,  

*Word analysis as at the first occurrence of this line.*

**ajjhattabahiddhā vā dhammesu dhammānupassī viharati.**

**Literal:** internally-and-externally or in-dhammas dhamma-contemplating he-dwells.

**Atthi dhammā'ti vā panassa sati paccupaṭṭhitā hoti yāvadeva ñāṇamattāya paṭissatimattāya,**

**Literal:** "There are dhammas" thus or, moreover for-him mindfulness present is, only-as-far-as for-the-measure-of-knowing, for-the-measure-of-mindfulness,  
**Faithful:** Or else mindfulness that "there are dhammas" is present in him only to the extent of bare knowing and continuous mindfulness,
