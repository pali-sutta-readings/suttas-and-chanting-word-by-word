# Dukkhasaccaniddesa

*Exposition of the Truth of Suffering*

### 4.5.1 Dukkhasaccaniddesa

*Exposition of the truth of suffering*

**Katamañca, bhikkhave, dukkhaṁ ariyasaccaṁ?**

**Literal:** What-and, bhikkhus, [is the] suffering noble-truth?  
**Faithful:** And what, bhikkhus, is the noble truth of suffering?  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`katamañca`** | `(sandhi)` [*katamaṁ* (interr pron nt nom sg) which?, what? + *ca* (ind) and] and what?; **[katamaṁ + ca]** |
