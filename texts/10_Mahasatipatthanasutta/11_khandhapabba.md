# Khandhapabba

*The Aggregates*

### 4.2 Dhammānupassanākhandhapabba

*Contemplation of dhammas: the section on the aggregates*

**Puna caparaṁ, bhikkhave, bhikkhu dhammesu dhammānupassī viharati pañcasu upādānakkhandhesu.**

**Literal:** Again and-further, bhikkhus, a-bhikkhu in-dhammas dhamma-contemplating dwells in-[the]-five clinging-aggregates.  
**Faithful:** Again, bhikkhus, a bhikkhu dwells contemplating dhammas as dhammas with regard to the five aggregates subject to clinging.

**Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu: iti rūpaṁ, iti rūpassa samudayo, iti rūpassa atthaṅgamo;**

**Literal:** Here, bhikkhus, a-bhikkhu: "thus form, thus of-form arising, thus of-form passing-away";  
**Faithful:** Here, bhikkhus, a bhikkhu contemplates: "Such is form, such is the arising of form, such is the passing away of form";

**iti ajjhattaṁ vā dhammesu dhammānupassī viharati,**

**Literal:** thus internally or in-dhammas dhamma-contemplating he-dwells,  
**Faithful:** in this way he dwells contemplating dhammas as dhammas internally,  

*Word analysis as at the first occurrence of this line.*

**bahiddhā vā dhammesu dhammānupassī viharati,**

**Literal:** externally or in-dhammas dhamma-contemplating he-dwells,  
**Faithful:** or he dwells contemplating dhammas as dhammas externally,  

*Word analysis as at the first occurrence of this line.*

**ajjhattabahiddhā vā dhammesu dhammānupassī viharati.**

**Literal:** internally-and-externally or in-dhammas dhamma-contemplating he-dwells.  
**Faithful:** or he dwells contemplating dhammas as dhammas both internally and externally.  

*Word analysis as at the first occurrence of this line.*

**Samudayadhammānupassī vā dhammesu viharati,**

**Literal:** Arising-nature-contemplating or in-dhammas he-dwells,  
**Faithful:** He dwells contemplating the nature of arising in dhammas,  

*Word analysis as at the first occurrence of this line.*

**vayadhammānupassī vā dhammesu viharati,**

**Literal:** vanishing-nature-contemplating or in-dhammas he-dwells,  
**Faithful:** or he dwells contemplating the nature of vanishing in dhammas,  

*Word analysis as at the first occurrence of this line.*

**samudayavayadhammānupassī vā dhammesu viharati.**

**Literal:** arising-vanishing-nature-contemplating or in-dhammas he-dwells.  
**Faithful:** or he dwells contemplating the nature of arising and vanishing in dhammas.  

*Word analysis as at the first occurrence of this line.*

**Atthi dhammā'ti vā panassa sati paccupaṭṭhitā hoti yāvadeva ñāṇamattāya paṭissatimattāya,**

**Literal:** "There are dhammas" thus or, moreover for-him mindfulness present is, only-as-far-as for-the-measure-of-knowing, for-the-measure-of-mindfulness,  
**Faithful:** Or else mindfulness that "there are dhammas" is present in him only to the extent of bare knowing and continuous mindfulness,  

*Word analysis as at the first occurrence of this line.*

**anissito ca viharati,**

**Literal:** independent and he-dwells,  
**Faithful:** and he dwells independent,  

*Word analysis as at the first occurrence of this line.*

**na ca kiñci loke upādiyati.**

**Literal:** not and anything in-the-world he-clings-to.  
**Faithful:** and he does not cling to anything in the world.  

*Word analysis as at the first occurrence of this line.*
