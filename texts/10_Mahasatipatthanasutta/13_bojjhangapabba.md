# Bojjhaṅgapabba

*The Factors of Awakening*

### 4.4 Dhammānupassanābojjhaṅgapabba

*Contemplation of dhammas: the section on the factors of awakening*

**Puna caparaṁ, bhikkhave, bhikkhu dhammesu dhammānupassī viharati sattasu bojjhaṅgesu.**

**Literal:** Again and-further, bhikkhus, a-bhikkhu in-dhammas dhamma-contemplating dwells in-[the]-seven awakening-factors.

**Evampi kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhu dhammesu dhammānupassī viharati sattasu bojjhaṅgesu.**

**Literal:** In-this-way-too indeed, bhikkhus, a-bhikkhu in-dhammas dhamma-contemplating dwells in-[the]-seven awakening-factors.  
**Faithful:** It is in this way too, bhikkhus, that a bhikkhu dwells contemplating dhammas as dhammas with regard to the seven factors of awakening.
