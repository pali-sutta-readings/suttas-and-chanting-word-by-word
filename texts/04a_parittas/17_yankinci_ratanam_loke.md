# Yaṅkiñci ratanaṁ loke

*Whatever Treasure There Is in the World*

**Yaṅkiñci ratanaṁ loke**

**Literal:** Whatever treasure in the world  
**Faithful:** Whatever treasure there may be in the world,  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`yaṅkiñci`** | `(rel pron nt nom sg)` whatever, any whatsoever; **[yaṁ + kiñci]** |
| **`ratanaṁ`** | `(nt nom sg)` treasure, jewel; **[ratana, no root in DPD]** |
| **`loke`** | `(masc loc sg)` in the world; **[√lok + a]** |

**vijjati vividhaṁ puthu**

**Literal:** is found, various, abundant,  
**Faithful:** found in many and various kinds,

| **`puthu`** | `(adj nt nom sg)` abundant, many, broad; **[√puth + u]** |
