# So attha-laddho

*May You Gain Your Aims*

### So attha-laddho

**So attha-laddho sukhito**

**Literal:** He, aim-gained, happy,  
**Faithful:** May he gain his aims and be happy,  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`so`** | `(dem pron masc nom sg)` he; **[ta]** |
| **`attha-laddho`** | `(adj masc nom sg comp)` [participle, read here as a wish] having gained [one's] aim, successful; **[attha + laddha]** |
| ↳ **`attha`** | `(masc comp)` aim, goal, benefit; **[√ar + tha]** |
| ↳ **`laddho`** | `(pp masc nom sg)` gained, obtained; **[√labh > ladh + ta]** |
| **`sukhito`** | `(pp masc nom sg)` [participle, read here as a wish] happy, at ease; **[√sukh + ita]** |

**viruḷho buddha-sāsane**

**Literal:** grown in [the] Buddha-teaching.  
**Faithful:** may he flourish in the Buddha's teaching.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`viruḷho`** | `(pp masc nom sg)` [participle, read here as a wish] grown, flourished; **[vi + √ruh + ta]** |
| **`buddha-sāsane`** | `(nt loc sg comp)` in the teaching of the Buddha, in the Buddha's dispensation; **[buddha + sāsana]** |
| ↳ **`buddha`** | `(masc comp)` Buddha, Awakened One; **[√budh + ta]** |
| ↳ **`sāsane`** | `(nt loc sg)` in the teaching, in the dispensation; **[√sās + ana]** |
