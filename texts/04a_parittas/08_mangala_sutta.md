# Maṅgala-sutta

*The Highest Blessings*

**Evam-me sutaṁ: ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā, sāvatthiyaṁ viharati, jeta-vane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme.**

**Literal:** Thus by me heard: one time [the] Blessed One at Sāvatthī is dwelling, in Jeta's Grove, in Anāthapiṇḍika's park.  
**Faithful:** Thus have I heard: at one time the Blessed One was staying near Sāvatthī, in Jeta's Grove, Anāthapiṇḍika's monastery.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |

| **`ekaṁ`** | `(card masc acc sg)` one; **[eka]** |

| **`bhagavā`** | `(masc nom sg)` Blessed One, Fortunate One; **[√bhaj > bhag + a + vant]** |
| **`sāvatthiyaṁ`** | `(fem loc sg)` at Sāvatthī, near Sāvatthī; **[√su > sāv + a + ttha + ī]** |
| **`viharati`** | `(3rd sg pres act indic)` [historic present] is staying, dwells; **[vi + √har + a + ti]** |

**Atha kho aññatarā devatā abhikkantāya rattiyā abhikkanta-vaṇṇā kevala-kappaṁ jetavanaṁ obhāsetvā, yena bhagavā ten'upasaṅkami.**
