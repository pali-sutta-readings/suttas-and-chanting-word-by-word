**Faithful:** May those who have fallen into suffering be free from suffering,

| :--- | :--- |

| ↳ **`ppattā`** | `(pp masc nom pl)` [*pattā* with doubled *p* in the compound] reached, fallen into; **[pa + √ap + ta]** |
| **`ca`** | `(ind)` and; **[ca]** |
