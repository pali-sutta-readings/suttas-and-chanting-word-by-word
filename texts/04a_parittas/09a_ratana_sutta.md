**Yaṅkiñci vittaṁ idha vā huraṁ vā**

**Literal:** Whatever wealth here or beyond or  
**Faithful:** Whatever wealth there is, here or beyond,  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |

| **`idha`** | `(adv)` here, in this world; **[ima + dha]** |
| **`vā`** | `(ind)` or; **[vā]** |
| **`huraṁ`** | `(ind)` beyond, in another world; **[huraṁ]** |
| **`vā`** | `(ind)` or; **[vā]** |
