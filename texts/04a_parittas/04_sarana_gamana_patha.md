# Saraṇa-gamana-pāṭha

*Going for Refuge*

**Buddhaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi**

**Literal:** [To the] Buddha [as] refuge I go  
**Faithful:** I go to the Buddha for refuge.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`buddhaṁ`** | `(masc acc sg)` Buddha, Awakened One; **[√budh + ta]** |
| **`saraṇaṁ`** | `(nt acc sg)` [predicate accusative] [as] refuge; **[√sar + aṇa]** |
| **`gacchāmi`** | `(1st sg pres act indic)` I go; **[√gam > gaccha + mi]** |
