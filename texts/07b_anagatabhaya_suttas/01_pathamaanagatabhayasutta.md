# Paṭhama-anāgatabhaya-sutta

*The First Discourse on Future Dangers*

**Pañcimāni, bhikkhave, anāgatabhayāni sampassamānena**

**Literal:** These five, bhikkhus, future dangers seeing,  
**Faithful:** Bhikkhus, seeing these five future dangers,  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
