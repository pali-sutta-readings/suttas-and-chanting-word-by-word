# Dvattiṁsākāra-pāṭha

*Reflection on the Thirty-Two Parts*

**Handa mayaṁ dvattiṁsākāra-pāṭhaṁ bhaṇāmase**

**Literal:** Come now, we [the] thirty-two-parts-passage let us recite  
**Faithful:** Now let us recite the passage on the thirty-two parts of the body.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`handa`** | `(interj)` come now, well then; **[handa]** |
| **`mayaṁ`** | `(1st pl nom)` we; **[ahaṁ]** |
| **`dvattiṁsākāra-pāṭhaṁ`** | `(masc acc sg comp)` passage on the thirty-two parts; **[dvattiṁsa + ākāra + pāṭha]** |
| ↳ **`dvattiṁsa`** | `(card comp)` thirty-two; **[dva + tiṁsā]** |
| ↳ **`ākāra`** | `(masc comp)` part, aspect; **[ā + √kar > kār + a]** |
| ↳ **`pāṭhaṁ`** | `(masc acc sg)` passage, text for recitation; **[√paṭh > pāṭh + a]** |
| **`bhaṇāmase`** | `(1st pl pres mid imperat)` let us recite, let us say; **[√bhaṇ + a + mase]** |
