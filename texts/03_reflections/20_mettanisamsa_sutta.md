# Mettānisaṁsa-sutta

*The Discourse on the Benefits of Loving-kindness*

**Handa mayaṁ mettānisaṁsa-suttaṁ bhaṇāmase**

**Literal:** Come now, we [the] loving-kindness-benefits-discourse let us recite  
**Faithful:** Come now, let us recite the discourse on the benefits of loving-kindness.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`handa`** | `(interj)` come now, well then; **[handa]** |
| **`mayaṁ`** | `(1st pl nom)` we; **[ahaṁ]** |
| **`mettānisaṁsa-suttaṁ`** | `(nt acc sg comp)` the discourse on the benefits of loving-kindness; **[mettā + ānisaṁsa + sutta]** |
| ↳ **`mett'`** | `(fem comp)` [*mettā* before a vowel] loving-kindness, goodwill; **[√mitt > mett + ā]** |
| ↳ **`ānisaṁsa`** | `(masc comp)` benefit, advantage; **[ānisaṁsa, no root in DPD]** |
| ↳ **`suttaṁ`** | `(nt acc sg)` discourse; **[√siv > suv + ta]** |
| **`bhaṇāmase`** | `(1st pl pres mid imperat)` let us recite, let us say; **[√bhaṇ + a + mase]** |

**Mettāya, bhikkhave, cetovimuttiyā āsevitāya bhāvitāya bahulīkatāya yānīkatāya vatthukatāya anuṭṭhitāya paricitāya susamāraddhāya ekādasānisaṁsā pāṭikaṅkhā.**

**Literal:** Of-loving-kindness, bhikkhus, liberation-of-mind pursued, developed, made-much-of, made-a-vehicle, made-a-basis, established, consolidated, well-undertaken, eleven-benefits [are] to-be-expected.  
**Faithful:** Bhikkhus, when the liberation of mind by loving-kindness is pursued, developed, cultivated, made a vehicle and a basis, established, consolidated and well undertaken, eleven benefits can be expected.
