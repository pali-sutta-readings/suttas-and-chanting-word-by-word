**Handa mayaṁ taṅkhaṇika-paccavekkhaṇa-pāṭhaṁ bhaṇāmase**

**Literal:** Come now, we [the] momentary-reflection-passage let us recite  
**Faithful:** Now let us recite the passage for reflection at the present moment.  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`handa`** | `(interj)` come now, well then; **[handa]** |
| **`mayaṁ`** | `(1st pl nom)` we; **[ahaṁ]** |
| **`taṅkhaṇika-paccavekkhaṇa-pāṭhaṁ`** | `(masc acc sg comp)` passage for reflection at the present moment; **[taṅkhaṇika + paccavekkhaṇa + pāṭha]** |

| ↳ **`pāṭhaṁ`** | `(masc acc sg)` passage, text for recitation; **[√paṭh > pāṭh + a]** |
