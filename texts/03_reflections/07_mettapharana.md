# Mettāpharaṇa

*Spreading Loving-Kindness*

**Handa mayaṁ mettāpharaṇaṁ karomase**

**Literal:** Come now, we [the] loving-kindness-pervading let us do  
**Faithful:** Now let us spread loving-kindness.

**Ahaṁ sukhito homi niddukkho homi, avero homi, abyāpajjho homi, anīgho homi, sukhī attānaṁ pariharāmi**

**Sabbe sattā kammassakā kammadāyādā kammayonī kammabandhū kammapaṭisaraṇā, yaṁ kammaṁ karissanti, kalyāṇaṁ vā pāpakaṁ vā, tassa dāyādā bhavissanti**

**Literal:** All beings kamma-owners, kamma-heirs, kamma-wombs, kamma-kinsmen, kamma-refuges; which kamma they will do, good or bad or, of that heirs they will be  
**Faithful:** All beings own their kamma; they are heirs to their kamma, born of kamma, bound to kamma, with kamma as their refuge; whatever kamma they do, whether good or bad, they will be its heirs.
