**Cha vo, bhikkhave, aparihāniye dhamme desessāmi,**

**Literal:** Six to you, bhikkhus, non-decline qualities I will teach;  
**Faithful:** Bhikkhus, I will teach you six qualities that do not lead to decline.

| :--- | :--- |
