# Saṅgaha-vatthu-gāthā

*Verses on the Bases of Support*

**Dānañ-ca peyya-vajjañ-ca**

**Literal:** Giving and, kind-speech and,  
**Faithful:** Giving and kind speech,  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`dānañ-ca`** | `(sandhi)` [sandhi of *dānaṁ* (nt nom sg) + *ca* (ind)] giving and; **[√dā + ana + ca]** |
| **`peyya-vajjañ-ca`** | `(sandhi)` [sandhi of *peyyavajjaṁ* (nt nom sg comp of *peyya*, kindness, + *vajja*, speech) + *ca* (ind)] kind speech and; **[√pī > pey + a + ya + √vad + ya + ca]** |
