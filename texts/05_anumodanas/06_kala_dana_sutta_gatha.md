# Kāla-dāna-sutta-gāthā

*Verses from the Discourse on Timely Giving*

**Kāle dadanti sapaññā**

**Literal:** At the right time give the wise,  
**Faithful:** The wise give at the right time,  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`kāle`** | `(masc loc sg)` at the right time, at the proper occasion; **[√kal > kāl + a]** |
| **`dadanti`** | `(3rd pl pres act indic)` give, offer; **[√dā > dadā + ti]** |
| **`sapaññā`** | `(masc nom pl)` wise, discerning; **[sa + pa + √ñā + ā + a]** |
