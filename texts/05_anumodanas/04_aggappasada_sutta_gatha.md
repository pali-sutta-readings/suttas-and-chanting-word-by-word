# Aggappasāda-sutta-gāthā

*Verses from the Discourse on Foremost Confidence*

**Aggato ve pasannānaṁ**

**Literal:** In the foremost truly of those confident,  
**Faithful:** For those with confidence in the foremost,  

| Word | Analysis & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`aggato`** | `(nt abl sg)` [ablative in *-to*, "with regard to"] in the foremost, as foremost; regarding the highest; **[agga + to; agga, no root in DPD]** |
| **`ve`** | `(ind)` indeed, truly; **[ve]** |
| **`pasannānaṁ`** | `(pp masc gen pl)` of those with confidence, of those who have faith; **[pa + √sad + na]** |
